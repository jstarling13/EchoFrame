## 2. Documents and order of precedence

This MSA governs signed SOWs for consulting, implementation, training, and knowledge transfer. The signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA) controls conflicts concerning covered personal-data processing. A signed change order controls the specifically identified scope or commercial terms it changes. A SOW controls its scope, price, timing, and acceptance criteria; it changes other MSA terms only by expressly identifying the clause and replacement language. Otherwise this MSA controls. Incorporated cancellation and travel policies supplement it and may not contradict a signed SOW. Website terms, proposals not incorporated into a signed SOW, purchase-order boilerplate, and online vendor terms do not amend it. Mandatory law remains controlling.

## 3. Authorization and delivery

The sales process is Fit call → Qualification → Proposal → Signed MSA/SOW → Private Stripe deposit invoice or payment link → Kickoff. There is no public self-serve checkout. Consultant begins only after execution, the initial payment, and agreed access prerequisites. Payment alone does not authorize deployment or access to sensitive data.

Each SOW identifies deliverables, included workflows, systems, meetings, training, dates, dependencies, acceptance tests, and support. Work not expressly included is excluded. Consultant will perform with reasonable professional care and skill and use suitably qualified personnel subject to confidentiality obligations. Neither party may bind the other to a third-party contract without authorization.

Client designates a decision-maker, supplies lawful access and reasonably accurate materials, obtains necessary employee/customer permissions, reviews outputs, and provides timely feedback. Delays caused by missing inputs require a documented schedule adjustment; additional fees require a signed change order, not automatic billing.

## 4. Change control and acceptance

A change request states the proposed change, business reason, added or removed deliverables, price, timeline, security implications, and signatories. Neither informal discussion nor an AI-generated recommendation changes scope. Authorized representatives must approve a change order before extra work begins.

Consultant submits each deliverable against the SOW's objective tests. Client has 10 business days after complete submission to accept in writing or identify material nonconformities with examples. Consultant will correct in-scope nonconformities without additional service fees and resubmit for review. Silence alone is not acceptance; the parties escalate unresolved review delays to their project leads. New preferences or scope are change requests. Unresolved material defects trigger the dispute and termination process. Production deployment always requires explicit written client approval even if deliverables are otherwise accepted.

## 9. Term and termination

This MSA continues until terminated by written notice; termination of the umbrella MSA does not silently cancel active SOWs. Client may terminate for convenience under the cancellation policy; O5's minimum-term treatment is expressly defined in its SOW and that policy.

At termination, Consultant stops unapproved work, provides a settlement statement, returns client data and access, and supplies paid work and an orderly handoff. Refunds cover unused advance payments less documented earned fees and approved unavoidable costs, without double recovery. Disputed balances do not authorize deletion of client materials. Confidentiality, accrued payment rights, IP rights, lawful liability limits, and dispute provisions survive as appropriate.
