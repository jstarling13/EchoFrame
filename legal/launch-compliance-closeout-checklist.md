## Governing Law / Venue — Decision Factors to Take to Counsel
- owner's domicile
- entity formation state
- principal place of business
- client geography
- where services are performed
- familiarity/cost of courts
- enforceability of contract provisions
- travel/onsite presence
- whether different client terms are necessary

## Stop Condition
Do not remove attorney-review-required labels merely because the forms look polished. Remove them only after actual professional review and owner acceptance.
