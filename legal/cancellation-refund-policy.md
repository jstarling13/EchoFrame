# EchoFrame Cancellation & Refund Policy (Business Draft)

**THIS IS A BUSINESS DRAFT, NOT LEGAL ADVICE. QUALIFIED COUNSEL SHOULD REVIEW BEFORE USE.**

## Billing model
All professional services are billed at $40/hour for actual hours worked. There is no upfront deposit and no fixed package to cancel out of.

## Free fit call
No charge, no cancellation terms apply.

## Paid discovery/diagnostic or implementation work
Once scheduled, only hours actually worked are billed. Canceling before work begins incurs no charge. Work already performed is billed regardless of later cancellation.

## Onsite travel
A confirmed trip's travel fee is non-refundable once booked (mirrors actual airfare/lodging cost exposure), unless canceled with enough notice that no cost was actually incurred. Cancellation notice terms: [COUNSEL TO SPECIFY].

## Ongoing hourly support
Month-to-month; either party may end the arrangement going forward. No minimum term unless a signed SOW says otherwise.

## Refunds
Because billing follows work performed rather than prepayment, refunds are limited to hours invoiced but not actually worked, or as otherwise required by law.

## Termination for cause
Termination rights for uncured material breach are governed by the MSA.
