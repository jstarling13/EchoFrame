## 2. Advance authorization

Remote delivery is the proposed default unless onsite work is expressly listed in the signed SOW. Before any client-billable booking, provide purpose, attendees, dates, origin/destination, estimated fares, lodging, ground travel, meals, any travel time, cancellation conditions and a not-to-exceed total. Client must approve in writing. Costs beyond the approved cap require fresh approval before commitment. No approval means no client-billable expense; Consultant may choose to incur its own nonbillable costs.

Travel is additional to the agreed service fee only when the signed SOW says so. Included expenses may not be billed twice. Onsite requests do not automatically expand implementation scope or meeting allocation.

## 3. Reimbursable costs

Subject to the approved budget, reimburse reasonable actual business travel costs supported by receipts, without markup unless a separately disclosed written agreement authorizes one. Use reasonably economical options consistent with the agreed schedule and safety. Personal upgrades, companions, entertainment, alcohol, fines, and unrelated travel are not reimbursable. No per diem is assumed; any per diem must state its amount and prevent duplicate reimbursement of actual meals/lodging.

Mileage reimbursement and fuel may not both be charged for the same vehicle miles where the mileage rate already covers fuel. Parking and tolls require itemization. Split trips serving multiple clients are allocated reasonably and disclosed; the same fare, mile or hour is not billed in full to multiple clients.

## 4. Travel time and records

Travel time is billable only if its rate, rounding method and cap are expressly approved in the SOW or travel authorization. Working time already billed as services is not also travel time. Expense statements identify project, traveler, date, purpose, currency, actual conversion method if applicable, receipt, approval reference and client allocation. Submit within [EXPENSE_SUBMISSION_PERIOD]. Payment follows the MSA's completed invoice terms. Applicable taxes must be separately and lawfully handled.

## 5. Cancellation, disruption and safety

If a visit is canceled, first seek refunds or credits and mitigate loss. Client pays only previously approved unavoidable costs allocated to its engagement; later refunds or credits benefiting the engagement are passed through or credited without double recovery. If Consultant cancels for its convenience, Consultant bears resulting avoidable charges. If Client cancels or changes dates, the cancellation policy governs earned service fees separately from travel costs.

Unsafe or materially disrupted travel may be rescheduled or replaced by remote delivery through written agreement. Neither party may require unsafe travel. Material schedule or scope changes use the MSA change-order process.
