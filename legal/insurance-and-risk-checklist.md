# EchoFrame Insurance & Risk Checklist

## Purpose
Questions for a licensed commercial insurance broker and counsel.

## Professional Liability / E&O
Ask:
- Does AI/workflow automation consulting fall within covered professional services?
- Are software configuration/integration errors covered?
- Are financial-workflow projects excluded or limited?
- Are contractual liability assumptions covered?
- What retroactive date applies?
- Are defense costs inside or outside limits?

## Cyber Liability
Ask whether policy covers client-data incidents involving cloud/AI vendors, social engineering, privacy claims, incident-response services, business interruption, regulatory defense where applicable, and contractual notification costs.

## General Liability
Ask whether client-site work creates a need for standard CGL coverage.

## Travel / Auto
Because onsite travel may occur, ask about personal auto coverage implications, hired/non-owned auto, rental cars, and client-site property damage.

## Equipment
Laptop/technology coverage during travel.

## Certificates
Ask whether larger clients are likely to require COIs, additional insured status, or specific minimum limits.

## Contract Alignment
Broker should review insurance requirements in the MSA before EchoFrame promises limits/coverages.

## Operational Risk Controls
Insurance does not replace MFA, least privilege, documented approvals, tested fallback, careful data handling, or scoped human review.
