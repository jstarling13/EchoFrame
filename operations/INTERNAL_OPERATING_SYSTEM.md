- active projects
- hours delivered
- unpaid admin hours
- travel
- invoices
- blockers

- case-study evidence

## Per-Project Source of Truth
One PROJECT_STATUS.md containing:
- objective
- client owner
- scope
- exclusions
- systems
- estimated hours
- hours used
- decisions
- risks
- next action
- approval needed

## Decision Log
Record:
- date
- decision
- who approved
- reason
- affected files/systems

## Prompt / Automation Versioning
For production AI workflows:
- version
- change
- test results
- release date
- rollback path

## Founder Capacity
Before accepting new work:
- current delivery hours
- travel commitments
- support burden
- hard deadlines
- military / personal availability constraints

Do not sell overlapping urgent implementations beyond realistic capacity.
