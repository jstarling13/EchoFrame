# EchoFrame Internal Metrics Dashboard Specification

## Sales
- introductions this month
- fit calls
- qualified opportunities
- proposals sent
- wins
- losses
- pipeline value estimated by expected hours

## Delivery
- active clients
- hours delivered
- average initial project hours
- hours by service family
- travel days
- rework hours
- support hours

## Financial
- invoiced revenue
- collected revenue
- unpaid invoices
- software/API cost
- travel cost
- contribution after direct project costs

## Referral
- introductions by partner
- conversion by partner
- repeat referrals
- partner response/feedback

## Outcome Evidence
- workflows measured before/after
- client-reported time reclaimed
- error/rework changes
- response-time changes
- documented testimonials/case studies approved for use

## Risk
- projects involving sensitive data
- projects waiting on access
- unresolved security/legal review
- production systems without documented handoff
