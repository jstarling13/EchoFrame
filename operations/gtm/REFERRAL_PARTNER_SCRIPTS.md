# EchoFrame Referral Partner Scripts

## Short Text to a Trusted Bookkeeper / CPA
I'm building EchoFrame around practical AI and workflow automation for small businesses. The best fit usually isn't a company saying "we need AI." It's a client where everyone already knows a process is too manual: matching deposits, chasing documents, reconciling information, building reports, copying between systems, or following up with customers.

I'm not trying to replace the bookkeeper or CPA. The goal is to take repetitive process work off their plate so they can focus on the judgment-heavy work.

If you have a client like that, a simple introduction is enough. I'll talk through the workflow and tell them if I think there's actually something worth fixing.

## More Direct Referral Ask
Do you have one client right now where you look at the process and think, "there is no reason they should still be doing this by hand"?

If so, that's the kind of introduction I'm looking for.

## Three-Way Intro Template for Partner
Jacob runs EchoFrame, which helps small businesses fix repetitive workflows using AI and automation. I thought of him because you mentioned [SPECIFIC PROBLEM]. He can talk through how the process works now and tell you whether it looks worth automating.

## Reply From Jacob
Thanks for the introduction. Before talking about tools, I'd like to understand the process as it works today. If it looks like something worth improving, I'll outline a practical next step. If an existing tool or process change is the better answer, I'll tell you that too.

## Partner Follow-Up After Intro
Thanks again for connecting us. I spoke with [CLIENT]. I appreciate you thinking of EchoFrame. I'll keep the engagement focused on the workflow you flagged and preserve the professional review points that belong with their bookkeeper/CPA.

Do not disclose confidential client details to the referrer without permission.

## Referral Partner Meeting Agenda
1. What kinds of manual work do your clients complain about?
2. Which tasks eat the most of your own time?
3. Where do clients consistently fail to send clean information?
4. Which workflows create recurring exceptions?
5. What would make you comfortable referring EchoFrame?
6. What would make you hesitate?
