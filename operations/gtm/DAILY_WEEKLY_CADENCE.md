# EchoFrame First-30-Day Sales Cadence

## Monday
- update target list
- identify 3–5 highest-value asks
- send/refine introductions
- review open proposals

## Tuesday
- fit calls
- same-day notes
- send next-step scopes

## Wednesday
- partner conversations
- work active delivery
- capture workflow language

## Thursday
- follow-ups
- proposal/diagnostic decisions
- request next introductions where appropriate

## Friday
30-minute GTM review:
- what moved?
- what stalled?
- which message worked?
- which workflow repeated?
- what should change next week?

## Rule
Do not spend five hours polishing marketing to avoid making five referral asks.
