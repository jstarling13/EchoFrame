# EchoFrame First 30 Days — Referral Channel Activation

## Objective
Turn the live site and referral positioning into actual conversations, paid diagnostic work, and early implementation proof.

This is an execution plan, not a revenue forecast.

# Week 1 — Build the Warm Map and Start Asking

## Day 1
Create the first 25-contact target sheet.

Priority order:
1. Jacob's aunt and the people she can introduce directly
2. bookkeepers/CPAs who already know Jacob or his family/professional network
3. clients in the aunt's network with visible manual workflow pain
4. adjacent trusted professionals who see operational problems:
   - fractional CFO/controller
   - business consultant
   - MSP/IT provider
   - business attorney
5. second-degree introductions in Queens/NYC

Do not scrape a list and call it warm outreach.

## Day 2
Prepare:
- one-sentence EchoFrame explanation
- partner intro text
- client intro text
- live website link
- one clear example workflow

## Days 3–5
Ask the first 5–8 warm referral partners for specific introductions.

Do not ask:
"Do you know anyone who needs AI?"

Ask:
"Do you have a client whose bookkeeping/admin team is still spending hours matching, copying, reconciling, chasing documents, or building reports by hand?"

# Week 2 — Run Conversations

Goal: get enough real conversations to test positioning.

For every call:
- identify workflow
- quantify rough frequency/time
- identify owner
- identify system(s)
- identify risk
- choose:
  - no fit
  - direct scope
  - paid diagnostic

Record the exact words prospects use.

Do not rewrite the website after every conversation. Track patterns first.

# Week 3 — Convert the Best Problems

For qualified prospects:
- send a short scope within 24 hours where possible
- use hour range
- identify required access
- identify success measure
- separate optional future work

For complex cases: sell the paid diagnostic rather than giving away detailed architecture.

Ask successful referrers:
"What kinds of workflow problems do you see most often across your clients?"

Use that answer to improve targeting.

# Week 4 — Review and Tighten

Review:
- which partner type produced introductions
- which wording got responses
- which workflows repeated
- which calls qualified
- which proposals stalled
- objections
- average expected project hours
- whether $40/hour caused concern in either direction
- whether paid diagnostic was understood
- which website pages prospects viewed/mentioned if analytics supports it

Then decide:
1. double down on best referral source
2. create one verified case study
3. build one industry-specific landing experience if enough evidence exists
4. refine, but do not completely reposition without evidence

# 30-Day Operating Targets
These are execution targets, not promises.

- 25 named warm/near-warm targets mapped
- 10–15 direct referral-partner asks
- 5–10 introductions or fit conversations
- 3–6 qualified opportunities
- 1–3 paid diagnostic/implementation starts
- at least one measurable project outcome in progress
- exact objections captured

If the network is smaller, prioritize quality over hitting the count.
