# EchoFrame Industry Content Map

# Accounting & Bookkeeping
## Common workflows
- deposit matching
- reconciliation support
- client document collection
- bill intake/routing
- month-end coordination
- exception queues
- reporting

## Message
Automation around professional judgment, not replacement of the professional.

# Professional Services
## Common workflows
- intake
- document organization
- recurring reporting
- scheduling/coordination
- internal knowledge
- follow-up

## Boundary
For regulated professions, EchoFrame focuses on administrative workflow, not licensed professional judgment.

# Real Estate & Property
## Common workflows
- tenant/admin intake
- remittance/payment workflow
- recurring communications
- document routing
- owner reporting
- multi-property visibility

# Construction & Trades
## Common workflows
- estimate intake
- job paperwork
- invoice/document routing
- lead follow-up
- status reporting
- recurring administrative handoffs

# Retail & Multi-Location
## Common workflows
- location reporting
- deposit/reconciliation support
- recurring admin
- exception reporting
- owner dashboards
- consistent process across locations

# Recruiting & Staffing
## Common workflows
- intake
- scheduling
- communications
- status reporting
- administrative coordination

## Boundary
Do not market autonomous employment decisions or consequential candidate screening.

# Multi-Entity Businesses
## Common workflows
- entity-level reporting
- intercompany tracking support
- document separation
- owner-level visibility
- reconciliation/admin coordination

## Boundary
Preserve legal/accounting entity separation and professional accounting review.

# Other
## Message
If the business has a recurring workflow that is repetitive, measurable, and stable enough to map, EchoFrame can evaluate whether automation is worth doing.
