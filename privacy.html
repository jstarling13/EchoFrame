Privacy Policy

Plain English, like everything else we do.

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect

The information you share with us.

When you sign up for a free sample report or become a subscriber, we collect the following:

  • Your name and email address — so we can send you your report and communicate with you about your account.
  • Your business financial data — specifically the P&L statements and supporting documents you choose to send us. We never request or require direct access to your accounting software, bank accounts, or any third-party system. You send us what you want us to see.
  • Basic usage information — standard analytics data about how you interact with this website (pages visited, time on site). This is collected automatically and does not identify you personally.
How we use it

What we do with your information.

We use your information for one purpose: to produce your monthly Clarity Report and communicate with you about it.

Specifically:

  • Your financial data is used only to generate your report and the benchmarks and recommendations inside it. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose other than producing your report.
  • Your email address is used to deliver your report and send occasional service-related updates. We do not send marketing emails to non-subscribers without your consent.
  • Your name is used to personalize your report. That's it.
Who sees your data

Your financials stay with us.

Your financial data is reviewed only by EchoFrame analysts involved in producing your report. We do not share your financial information with any third party, advertiser, partner, or outside service.

The only exception: if we are required to disclose information by law or valid legal process, we will comply with that requirement and notify you where legally permitted to do so.

How we store it

How your data is kept.

Your financial documents are stored securely and are retained only as long as needed to produce your reports and maintain your account history. If you cancel your subscription and request deletion of your data, we will permanently delete your financial documents within 30 days.

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security — but we take this seriously and treat your financial data with the same care we would want applied to our own.

Your rights

You're in control.

  • You can request a copy of the data we hold about you at any time.
  • You can request deletion of your data at any time.
  • You can unsubscribe from communications at any time.
  • You can cancel your subscription at any time with no penalty.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at jacobstarling4313@gmail.com and we will respond within 5 business days.
