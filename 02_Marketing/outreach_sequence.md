# Outreach Sequence — Home Services (HVAC, Plumbers, Electricians)

This is the sequence to run after pulling the Apollo prospect list. Five emails over 21 days plus 2 LinkedIn touches. Send the LinkedIn connection request the same day as Email 1. Send the LinkedIn DM the day after they accept.

Replace bracketed placeholders before sending. Personalize the first sentence whenever possible — a one line nod to something specific (their truck wrap, their reviews, a recent job they posted) doubles reply rates.

## Email 2 — Day 3: The Problem

**Goal:** Educate on the pain point. Name 2 to 3 specific financial leaks common to home service businesses. Soft CTA.

**Subject:** Where do most HVAC businesses leak money?

[First Name],

Three places we see cash leak in almost every HVAC shop we look at:

1. Materials markup not tracked per job. Average shop is leaving $1,500 to $2,500 a month on the table because they don't know which jobs hit target margin and which don't.
2. The "misc" expense bucket. Anything over 2% of revenue is hiding 4 to 6 line items that should be tracked separately. Usually one or two of them are recurring charges that should have been canceled six months ago.
3. Fuel without a fleet card. A growing crew burns $400 to $600 a month they don't need to.

We can show you exactly which of these apply to [Company Name] in a free sample report. Worth a look?

[Your Name]

## Email 3 — Day 7: Social Proof

**Goal:** Make it feel real. Describe what an owner found in their first report and acted on. Specific numbers.

**Subject:** What HVAC owners find in their first report

[First Name],

A Birmingham HVAC owner I worked with last month opened his first report and saw three things he hadn't seen before:

- His materials were running 25% of revenue but he had no way to tell which job types were hitting his 35% markup target. He started tracking job level cost the same week.
- His "misc" line was $2,800 a month. He found a software subscription nobody had used in eight months and a fuel card that was double charging him.
- His net margin was 27% on paper but his cash position kept shrinking. The report flagged that his receivables aging had quietly grown to $42,000 over 60 days. He made collection calls and got $18,000 in within three weeks.

That was the first 30 minutes of one report. Same exercise for [Company Name] is free if you want it.

## Notes for Sending

- **Send window:** Tuesday through Thursday, 7am to 9am local. Avoids the dispatch crunch.
- **Reply rate target:** 5 to 8% across the full sequence. If you're under 3% after 100 sends, the subject lines or opening lines need work, not the offer.
- **Don't auto send Email 5 if they've replied.** Even a "no thanks" gets them moved to a separate list.
- **Track everything in the client tracker.** Every send, reply, and call gets logged so you can see what's working.
- **Personalization is the multiplier.** A 30 second look at their website or Google reviews to add a one line opener (e.g., "Saw you've got 80+ five star reviews — clearly doing something right operationally") doubles reply rates compared to fully generic.
