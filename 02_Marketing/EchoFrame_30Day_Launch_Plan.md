# EchoFrame — 30-Day Launch Plan (Sweat-Equity Edition)

**Goal for the next 30 days:** land your **first 2–3 paying Monthly Clarity clients** ($150/mo each) and collect **2 testimonials** you can use in every ad and post afterward.

This plan assumes ~10–15 founder hours/week, near-zero ad spend, and runs *alongside* the paid ads and the Instagram/Facebook intro post you're already doing. It does not repeat your existing ad/referral plan — it's the free, founder-led motion that gets the first clients fastest.

---

## The one idea this plan is built on

Your free sample report is the most powerful sales tool you have. It's a $0-risk, personalized, genuinely valuable artifact that no competitor hands out. So the entire game for 30 days is simple:

> **Get as many of the right Columbus owners as possible to say "yes, build me a free sample" — then convert those samples into $150/mo.**

Everything below feeds that one sentence. Don't sell the subscription cold. Sell the *free sample*. The sample sells the subscription for you.

And because the sample is delivered by you manually within 48 hours, **none of this is blocked by the email setup.** You can start today.

---

## Your funnel (keep it this simple)

**Reach an owner → they request a free sample → you deliver it in 48h → 15-min "One Thing" call → $150/mo.**

Track only these five numbers on a sticky note or a sheet:

| Metric | 30-day target |
|---|---|
| Owners reached (DM / email / walk-in / call) | 150 |
| Free samples requested | 20 |
| Samples delivered (within 48h) | 20 |
| "One Thing" calls booked | 8 |
| Paying clients closed | 2–3 |

If reach is high but sample requests are low → your pitch/offer needs work. If samples are high but closes are low → your follow-up/close needs work. The numbers tell you where to fix.

## Channel playbook — ranked by speed to your first client

### 1. Warm network first (fastest possible clients — do this Day 1)

You know people who own Columbus businesses, or who *work for* one, or whose family does. These are your warmest, highest-trust leads and they'll give you your first testimonials.

- List every Columbus business owner you know personally, plus every friend/family member who works for or is related to one.
- Don't pitch a subscription. Offer the free sample as a favor + a favor back: *"I'm launching this and I want real reps — can I build your shop a free report? If it's useful, a 2-line testimonial would mean the world."*

### 2. Hyper-local direct outreach (your real moat)

National tools can't walk into a Columbus restaurant. You can. This is where founder-led, local, $150/mo wins.

- **Walk-ins:** Visit restaurants, salons, auto shops, and trades businesses at *off-peak* hours (2–4pm for food). Ask for the owner. Hand them a one-page leave-behind. Offer the free sample. Even a 20% "yes, sure" rate compounds fast.
- **Instagram DMs:** Local restaurants and salons run their own IG. A warm, specific DM that references their actual business gets read because it's *not* a mass blast.
- **Email:** For trades/auto/B2B where IG is weaker, short personal emails to the owner.

Aim for ~8–10 personalized touches a day. Quality over volume — every message should name their business and feel hand-written (because it is).

### 3. Organic social — make the sample do the selling

You've started with the intro post. Keep a steady cadence (3 posts/week across IG + FB) that *proves the value* rather than just announcing you exist:

- **"One leak I found" posts (your best content):** Anonymized, no client named — *"A Columbus restaurant was running 4 points over on food cost. That's ~$1,200/month walking out the door. Here's how it showed up in the numbers."* This is the single most persuasive thing you can post.
- **Local benchmark posts:** *"What should labor cost be for a Columbus salon? Here's the range I'm seeing."* Positions you as the local expert.
- **Founder story / behind-the-scenes:** Why you built it, the Emory finance background, "every report comes through me." Builds the trust that justifies $150/mo.
- **The offer, stated plainly, ~1 in 4 posts:** *"Free sample report on your actual numbers, 48h, no card. Comment SAMPLE or DM me."*

Always end value posts with a soft CTA to the free sample. Pin the intro post.

### 4. Referral partners (compounding, start in week 2)

Some local professionals touch owners' finances but *don't* do advisory — they're natural referral partners, not competitors:

- **Bookkeepers** (they do the books, not the "what to do about it")
- **Commercial insurance agents** and **commercial realtors** (sell to owners all day)
- **POS / booking software reps** (Toast, Square, etc. local reps)
- **Business bankers** at local branches

Pitch: *"We don't compete — you handle X, I translate their numbers into one action a month. If you ever have a client who's stressed about money, I'll build them a free sample, no strings. Happy to send referrals back your way too."*

### 5. Local groups & Google (set-and-forget basics, week 1)

- **Google Business Profile** for EchoFrame (Columbus, GA) — free, and it's where people check legitimacy.
- **Local Facebook groups** (Columbus GA small business / entrepreneur / community groups). Don't spam — answer money questions helpfully, mention the free sample when relevant.

## Turning a free sample into a paying client (the close)

The sample is where you win, so don't just email it and hope. Run this every time:

1. **Deliver within 48h, exactly as promised.** Speed = credibility.
2. **Don't just send the file — book a 15-minute call** to "walk you through the One Thing I found." On the call, show the single leak tied to a dollar amount, then: *"That's one month. I do this every month for $150 — and if a report ever doesn't find something actionable, you don't pay. Want me to set up next month?"*
3. **The guarantee does the heavy lifting** — lead with it. It removes 100% of their risk.
4. **If not now, ask permission to follow up** next month with a fresh leak. Most "no"s are "not yet."

## 30-day week-by-week

**Week 1 — Foundations + warm list**
- Write your warm list (everyone you know who owns/works-for/is-related-to a Columbus business).
- Set up Google Business Profile; join 3–4 local FB groups.
- Make a one-page leave-behind for walk-ins (offer + guarantee + "free sample" CTA + your contact).
- Reach out to your entire warm list. Goal: first 3–5 sample requests this week.
- Keep posting (intro post is live; add one "one leak I found" post).

**Week 2 — Direct outreach engine on**
- 8–10 personalized touches/day (walk-ins + IG DMs + emails). Goal: 40+ owners reached.
- Deliver every sample within 48h; book a call with each.
- Start 3 referral-partner conversations.
- 3 posts (1 value, 1 local benchmark, 1 offer).

**Week 3 — Convert + referral partners**
- Keep outreach at pace; push to first paying client(s).
- Lock in 2–3 referral partners; give them your one-pager + free-sample offer.
- Ask your first happy client for a 2-line testimonial — use it everywhere immediately.
- 3 posts (lead with the testimonial if you have it).

**Week 4 — Double down on what's working**
- Look at your five numbers. Which channel produced sample requests cheapest (in time)? Pour more hours there.
- Follow up with every "not yet" from weeks 1–3 with a fresh angle.
- Tee up month 2: the channel that converted best becomes your main motion, and feed your best "one leak" content into the paid ads you're running.

## Where to spend your 10–15 hours/week

- **~50% outreach** (warm list, walk-ins, DMs, emails) — this is what produces clients in month 1.
- **~30% delivering samples + close calls** — the actual product work; protect the 48h promise.
- **~15% content** (3 posts/week + group engagement).
- **~5% referral-partner conversations.**

Paid ads run in the background; don't let them eat the hours that produce your first clients. Outreach beats ads for getting from 0 → first few clients.

---

## The two things that will make or break month 1

1. **Volume of the *right* asks.** 150 genuine, personalized touches to Columbus owners offering a free sample will produce clients. 15 will not. This is a numbers game powered by a great offer.
2. **Sample quality + speed.** Every sample that lands in 48h with a real, specific, dollar-tied leak is a near-guaranteed close and a likely testimonial. The product is your best marketer — make each sample undeniable.
