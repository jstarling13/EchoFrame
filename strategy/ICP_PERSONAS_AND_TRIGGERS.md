**Purpose:** Focus acquisition without permanently narrowing the company

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Ideal company

10-250 employees; established revenue; recurring document/email/spreadsheet work; visible backlog or service bottleneck; owner or operator can sponsor change; manageable IT/security path; willingness to assign process owners.

## Personas

**Owner/CEO:** wants capacity, consistency, and visibility without another opaque vendor. **COO/operations leader:** owns throughput and adoption. **Department manager:** feels the daily pain and fears disruption. **IT/security:** needs data path, access, vendor, and rollback clarity. **Finance:** requires credible baseline and benefit classification.

## Strong pains

Repeated re-keying; inconsistent client communications; slow document drafting/review; tribal knowledge; handoff errors; overloaded managers; long onboarding; scattered templates; poor follow-up; weak reporting; vendor sprawl.

## Triggers

New system rollout; growth without proportional hiring; backlog; key employee departure; acquisition/integration; margin pressure; service-quality failures; leadership mandate; competitor adoption; renewal of major software; audit/security event.

## Disqualifiers

No executive sponsor; no process owner; request to evade policy/law; expectation of unsupervised consequential decisions; unwillingness to provide baseline access; no budget; desire for “magic prompts” without process change.
