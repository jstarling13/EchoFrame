# EchoFrame ICP, Personas & Buying Triggers — Revised

## Strategic Recommendation
Use a two-lane ICP so launch messaging stays focused while the business does not artificially reject larger opportunities.

# Lane A — Primary Launch ICP

## Company Profile
Recommended range:
- approximately 2–50 employees
- owner-led or tightly managed
- service-heavy or administrative-work-heavy
- at least one recurring process that touches multiple tools or people
- able to make a buying decision without enterprise procurement
- enough revenue / operating maturity to pay for professional services
- willing to provide access to the real process and a subject-matter owner

## Strong Fit Signals
- "We do this manually every day."
- "Only one person knows how this works."
- "We are copying the same information between systems."
- "Reconciliation takes too long."
- "We are behind on follow-up."
- "I have no clean view across my entities."
- "We hired more people because the process is inefficient."
- "My bookkeeper/office manager is buried."
- "We tried AI, but it never became part of the workflow."

## Buyer Personas

### Owner / Managing Partner
Wants:
- more visibility
- less operational drag
- faster execution
- fewer hidden dependencies

Objections:
- fear of disruption
- uncertainty about ROI
- concern that AI is hype
- data/security concerns

### Bookkeeper / CPA / Finance Professional
Role:
Often the trusted introducer or process expert.

Wants:
- fewer repetitive tasks
- better source data
- cleaner exception handling
- capacity to serve more clients
- preserved professional judgment

Do not position EchoFrame as replacing this persona.

### Office / Operations Manager
Wants:
- fewer manual handoffs
- better process consistency
- less chasing
- documentation that survives staff changes

### Finance / Accounting Lead
Wants:
- cleaner workflows
- better reconciliation support
- clearer controls
- traceable changes
- no autonomous accounting judgments without review

## Buying Triggers
- key employee overloaded or leaving
- business adds locations/entities
- backlog becomes visible
- owner sees labor growing faster than revenue
- accounting/bookkeeping process becomes a bottleneck
- new software rollout
- recurring service errors
- lead response problems
- reporting delay
- acquisition or entity complexity
- trusted advisor identifies a workflow problem

## Disqualifiers
- no decision-maker
- no process owner
- expectation of "fully autonomous" high-risk decisions
- unwillingness to provide process access
- demand for guaranteed savings
- request to bypass controls or professional review
- project requires regulated infrastructure EchoFrame is not prepared to support
- client expects indefinite support without paying for it
- client wants a custom build where an inexpensive proven tool already solves the problem

# Lane B — Expansion ICP

## Profile
Approximately 51–250 employees, provided:
- executive sponsor exists
- process owner is assigned
- project can be bounded
- security / procurement requirements are manageable
- EchoFrame can deliver responsibly without overstating its scale

## Use
Do not make this the center of launch messaging. Pursue opportunistically and through strong referrals.

# Geographic Priority
Initial emphasis:
- New York City / Northeast referral network
- remote delivery wherever practical
- onsite travel only where justified and separately quoted
