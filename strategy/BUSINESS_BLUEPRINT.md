**Purpose:** Define how the company creates, delivers, and captures value

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Mission

Help established businesses turn repeatable knowledge work into safe, useful AI-enabled workflows, then give their people the skills and documentation to own them.

## Business model

Founder-led projects produce diagnostic, implementation, training, and assurance revenue. Productized boundaries reduce sales friction while SOWs preserve flexibility. Retainers protect workflow reliability and adoption after launch.

## Category

AI Operations Enablement sits between generic training, management consulting, automation agencies, and software vendors. The unit of value is an adopted workflow with an owner, controls, evidence, and measurable operating impact.

## Operating loop

Discover → map → classify data → score opportunities → design → prototype → test → train → launch → measure → transfer → improve.

## Moat

Cumulative workflow patterns, decision records, test libraries, role-based training, vendor-neutral selection evidence, and credible ROI measurement. Client dependency is not the moat.

## Success metrics

Qualified referral introductions; diagnostic-to-build conversion; gross margin; time-to-first-value; workflow adoption; acceptance pass rate; verified hours/cost/revenue effects; retainer attachment; referral and case-study rate.
