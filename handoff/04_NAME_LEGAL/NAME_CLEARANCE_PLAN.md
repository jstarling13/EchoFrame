# Name Clearance Plan

**Purpose:** Treat selection as provisional until relevant conflicts are evaluated

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-29

## Search

Federal exact/similar marks and coordinated services; Georgia entities and state marks; New York entities; internet/common-law businesses; LinkedIn/directories; domains/social handles.

## Evaluate

Similarity in appearance/sound/meaning, related services/channels/customers, geographic reach, active use, priority dates, strength of “White Oak,” and ability to distinguish. Exact-match absence is not clearance.

## Professional gate

Trademark attorney reviews material conflicts and recommends use/application strategy. Do not claim clearance based only on automated or entity-database results.

## Implementation

Keep local/Preview name; do not purchase expensive collateral, publish Production, file entity/mark, or connect permanent domain until owner accepts clearance risk with counsel.
