# Owner Visual Review

**Purpose:** Judge the Preview as a business, not only a build

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-29

## Brand

Does White Oak feel established, premium, trustworthy, and appropriate for traditional owners? Does it look like a serious consultancy rather than a generic AI startup?

## Clarity

Can a visitor understand advise/build/teach, ideal client, ownership transfer, first engagement, price, risk boundary, and next step within two minutes?

## Conversion

Book-a-fit-call CTA clear; no accidental self-serve buying; contact burden reasonable; confirmation sets expectations; mobile navigation/footer/contact complete.

## Decision

Approve, approve with named fixes, or reject. Record screenshots, page, issue, severity, owner, and acceptance evidence.
