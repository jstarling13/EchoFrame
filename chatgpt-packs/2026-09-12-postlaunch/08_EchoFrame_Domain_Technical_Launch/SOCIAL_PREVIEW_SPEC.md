# EchoFrame Social Preview Specification

## Goal
Shared links should look deliberate on LinkedIn, iMessage, Slack, Teams, and other platforms.

## Primary Open Graph Image
Recommended ratio: 1200 × 630

## Visual Direction
Use EchoFrame's own brand, not Bain's.

Suggested composition:
- clean off-white/light background
- EchoFrame logo
- one concise line: **AI Implementation & Workflow Automation**
- restrained use of current EchoFrame palette
- generous whitespace
- no tiny body copy
- no fake client logos
- no stock collage

## Variants
### Home
EchoFrame  
AI Implementation & Workflow Automation

### Services
EchoFrame Services  
Practical Automation for Real Business Operations

### Industries
EchoFrame Industries  
Workflows Built Around How the Business Operates

## Implementation
Prefer Next.js App Router metadata/file conventions appropriate to the current repo, including Open Graph and social-card metadata and page-specific imagery where valuable.

## QA
Test social-preview cropping and readability on common desktop/mobile unfurl contexts.
