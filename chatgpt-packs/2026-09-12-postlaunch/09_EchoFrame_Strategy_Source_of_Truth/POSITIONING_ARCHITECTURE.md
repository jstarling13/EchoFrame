# EchoFrame Positioning Architecture

## 1. Audience
### Primary
Owner-led / closely managed businesses, generally 2–50 employees.

### Expansion
51–250 employee organizations with clear sponsor, process owner, manageable governance, and bounded scope.

## 2. Trigger Problem
The business has a recurring workflow that is too manual, spread across systems, dependent on one person, causing backlog, creating rework, slowing customer response, or producing poor visibility.

## 3. Category
AI implementation and workflow automation consulting.

## 4. Promise
A practical, measurable improvement to the actual workflow, not a generic AI demonstration.

## 5. Mechanism
Public: **OWNED Method**

Internal: Discover → Map → Measure → Prioritize → Design → Build → Test → Train → Transfer → Improve

## 6. Commercial Simplicity
Free fit conversation.

Substantive discovery/diagnostic: paid at standard rate.

Professional services: $40/hour.

Travel: quoted separately.

Onsite food stipend: $50/day.

No public package menu. No public checkout.

## 7. Evidence
Show before/after process, measured capacity, exception handling, time/cycle, quality/rework, response time, and client usability.

Do not claim speculative headcount savings or guaranteed financial returns.

## 8. Differentiators
- founder does the work
- transparent economics
- workflow-first
- tool-agnostic
- AI only where justified
- client-owned result
- bookkeeper/CPA partnership stance
- explicit human review
- documented handoff

## 9. Messaging Hierarchy
### What
AI implementation and workflow automation.

### Problem
Too much valuable staff time is trapped in repetitive work.

### How
Map the workflow, isolate the routine path, design controls, implement, test, and transfer.

### Why EchoFrame
Direct implementation without unnecessary layers or vendor lock-in.

## 10. CTA Ladder
1. free fit conversation
2. paid discovery if needed
3. scoped implementation
4. measured result
5. expansion only when justified
6. optional ongoing hourly support

## 11. Boundary
EchoFrame does not market autonomous legal, medical, investment, lending, employment, or other high-impact professional decisions.
