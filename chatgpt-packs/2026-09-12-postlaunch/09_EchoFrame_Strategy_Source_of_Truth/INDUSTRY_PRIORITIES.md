# EchoFrame Industry Priorities

## Principle
Industry matters because workflows, systems, regulation, and trust differ. EchoFrame should still prioritize the workflow problem over forcing every prospect into a vertical.

# Wave 1 — Immediate

## Accounting & Bookkeeping-Adjacent
Why:
- strongest warm referral channel
- repeated financial workflows
- direct relevance to existing proof
- strong partner-not-replacement story

Common opportunities:
- deposit matching
- reconciliation support
- document intake
- client request lists
- bill routing
- month-end coordination
- exception handling
- reporting

## Professional Practices
Examples: medical-practice administration, legal-office administration, consulting/professional services, multi-location practices.

Boundary: administrative workflow only where regulated professional judgment is involved.

## Multi-Entity Owner / Operators
Common opportunities:
- entity-level status
- intercompany tracking support
- document routing
- consolidated management visibility while preserving entity separation

## Commercial Real Estate / Property Administration
Common opportunities:
- tenant intake
- payment/remittance workflow
- owner reporting
- document routing
- recurring communication
- admin coordination

# Wave 2 — After Repeatable Proof

## Construction & Specialty Contractors
- estimate/job intake
- invoice routing
- document collection
- status reporting
- lead follow-up

## Recruiting & Staffing
- intake
- scheduling
- communication
- reporting
- administrative coordination

Boundary: no autonomous consequential employment decisions.

## Architecture / Engineering / B2B Services
- project administration
- proposal support
- document workflow
- recurring reporting
- internal knowledge retrieval

## Retail / Multi-Location Operations
- recurring reporting
- deposit/reconciliation support
- recurring admin
- exception reporting
- owner dashboards
- consistent process across locations

# Gate / Do Not Actively Market Without Additional Capability
- clinical decision support
- PHI-heavy AI processing without approved architecture
- legal advice generation
- lending/underwriting
- investment recommendation
- autonomous hiring/screening
- biometric surveillance
- safety-critical control
- autonomous financial commitments

# Website Industry Organization
Recommended public selector categories:
- Accounting & Bookkeeping
- Professional Services
- Real Estate & Property
- Construction & Trades
- Retail & Multi-Location
- Recruiting & Staffing
- Multi-Entity Businesses
- Other

Do not overbuild separate vertical pages until enough real work exists to support meaningful industry-specific proof.
