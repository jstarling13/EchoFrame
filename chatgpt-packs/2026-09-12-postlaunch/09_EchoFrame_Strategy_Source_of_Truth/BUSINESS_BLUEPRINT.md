# EchoFrame Business Blueprint

## 1. Business Definition
EchoFrame is a founder-led AI implementation and workflow automation consulting practice.

EchoFrame helps small and lower-mid-market businesses remove repetitive administrative work, improve operational visibility, connect fragmented workflows, and apply AI where it creates practical business value.

The business is implementation-led. Strategy exists to determine what should be built and what should not.

## 2. Current Stage
The marketing site is live in production. The immediate priority is client activation, legal/compliance completion, referral-channel development, and disciplined delivery.

## 3. Commercial Model
- free initial fit conversation
- paid diagnostic/discovery when detailed mapping, analysis, or technical design is required
- $40/hour professional services
- travel quoted separately
- $50/day onsite food stipend
- approved third-party costs handled transparently
- no fixed public packages
- no public checkout
- optional ongoing hourly support

## 4. Primary Launch Market
Owner-led and closely managed small businesses, generally 2–50 employees, where:
- a buyer is identifiable
- a process owner exists
- manual work is visible
- one or more recurring workflows have meaningful time/error/capacity cost
- the business can provide system/process access
- buying does not require enterprise procurement

Primary acquisition motion: trusted bookkeeper/CPA/professional-advisor referrals, initially with strong emphasis on the New York / Northeast network.

## 5. Expansion Market
Established organizations, roughly 51–250 employees, can be pursued when:
- an executive sponsor exists
- process ownership is clear
- governance/security requirements can be supported
- scope is bounded
- founder-led delivery remains realistic

This lane is opportunistic rather than the center of launch marketing.

## 6. Problems EchoFrame Solves
- repetitive data entry
- manual reconciliation support
- document intake/routing
- multi-entity administrative complexity
- spreadsheet-heavy operations
- email-driven workflows
- slow lead/customer follow-up
- recurring manual reporting
- fragmented information
- process dependency on one employee
- inconsistent AI usage
- poor handoff/documentation

## 9. Value Logic
A project is attractive when measurable labor capacity is reclaimed, errors/rework decline, response speed improves, visibility improves, backlog declines, the client can avoid unnecessary hiring/vendor spend, or the process becomes less dependent on one individual.

Recovered time is capacity, not automatically cash savings. EchoFrame should not make guaranteed-savings claims.

## 10. Differentiation
- direct founder involvement
- practical operational focus
- transparent billing
- willingness to say when AI is unnecessary
- preference for existing client systems where adequate
- explicit human-review points
- client ownership and transferability
- bookkeeping/financial workflow fluency
- current knowledge of AI tools without model hype

## 11. What EchoFrame Is Not
- managed IT provider
- generic AI prompt agency
- SaaS product catalog
- replacement for bookkeepers/CPAs
- legal/tax/accounting advisor
- clinical AI provider
- autonomous high-risk decision system
- 24/7 help desk

## 12. Operating Principles
1. Map before building.
2. Quantify before expanding scope.
3. Automate the routine path; surface exceptions.
4. Keep professional judgment human.
5. Prefer simple systems.
6. Test failure modes.
7. Document and transfer.
8. Avoid client lock-in.
9. Measure results.
10. Decline work that is unsafe, uneconomic, or outside capability.
