# EchoFrame Referral Intro / Fit Call Playbook

## Goal
Qualify the workflow, not sell AI.

## Opening
"Tell me what the process looks like today. I want to understand where the work actually happens before talking about tools."

## Questions
1. What starts the workflow?
2. Who touches it?
3. What systems are involved?
4. What gets copied or re-entered?
5. How often?
6. How long does it take?
7. Where does it break or wait?
8. What requires judgment?
9. What happens when it is wrong or late?
10. Who owns the process?
11. Who needs to approve a change?
12. What sensitive data is involved?
13. What has already been tried?
14. What would a useful result look like?

## Close
### Clear / Small
"I can scope the first implementation from this."

### Complex
"The right next step is paid discovery. I'll map the workflow, systems, exceptions, and controls, then come back with the implementation scope before building."

### No Fit
"I don't think custom automation is the best answer here. I'd rather tell you that now than create a project that doesn't earn its cost."

## Follow-Up SLA
Send the fit outcome, next step, and scope/diagnostic outline within one business day when possible.

## Sales Rule
Never give away hours of architecture on the free fit call.
