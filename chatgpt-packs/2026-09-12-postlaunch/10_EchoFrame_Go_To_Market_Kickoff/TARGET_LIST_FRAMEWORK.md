# EchoFrame Target List Framework

## Tier 0 — Existing Live Opportunity / Active Prospect
Anyone already discussing a real workflow.

Track:
- exact problem
- next decision
- last contact
- proposal status
- referrer

## Tier 1 — Direct Trusted Referral Partners
People who can make a warm introduction today.

Priority:
- aunt/bookkeeper network
- CPAs
- bookkeepers
- controllers
- trusted business advisors

## Tier 2 — Warm Business Owners
Owners Jacob or a trusted connector already knows.

High-fit signals:
- multiple entities
- manual financial admin
- office manager overloaded
- recurring reports built manually
- leads not followed up
- multi-location operations

## Tier 3 — Second-Degree Referral Partners
A trusted person can introduce Jacob to:
- bookkeeping firms
- CPA firms
- fractional finance leaders
- IT/MSPs
- business consultants

## Tier 4 — Carefully Selected New Partners
Only after warm lanes are being worked.

Find professionals in the target geography/vertical who:
- serve many small businesses
- do not sell the same implementation service
- have a reputation to protect
- can recognize workflow pain

Approach as a partner conversation, not a generic AI pitch.

# Required Fields
- name
- company
- role
- tier
- city
- relationship source
- partner/prospect
- likely workflow
- date added
- intro asked?
- intro made?
- last contact
- next action
- status
- notes
