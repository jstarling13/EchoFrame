## 2. Framework
The MSA governs one or more Statements of Work (SOWs). Each SOW defines the specific services, scope, deliverables, assumptions, rates, and timing.

If an SOW conflicts with the MSA, counsel should define which document controls and for what subject matter.

## 3. Services
EchoFrame provides consulting, workflow design, AI/automation implementation, training, and related professional services described in each SOW.

EchoFrame does not provide legal, accounting, tax, medical, investment, underwriting, or other licensed professional advice unless separately qualified and explicitly contracted, which is not the current business model.

## 4. Fees
Current business model:
- $40/hour for professional services
- travel quoted in writing
- $50/day onsite food stipend
- third-party software/API/vendor costs only as approved
- invoicing cadence: [COUNSEL/OWNER TO SET]
- payment terms: [COUNSEL/OWNER TO SET]

No SOW should imply a fixed-price package unless expressly written.

## 5. Estimates
Hour estimates are planning estimates, not fixed fees.
EchoFrame should notify the client before materially exceeding an agreed estimate range when the overage results from scope changes, unforeseen data/system conditions, access constraints, or client-requested additions.

## 6. Client Responsibilities
Client is responsible for providing authorized access, identifying decision-makers/process owners, supplying accurate business rules, obtaining required professional advice, reviewing high-impact decisions, approving production launch, maintaining credentials/accounts under client control, and providing timely feedback/access.

## 7. Change Control
Material scope changes should be documented in writing and may alter timing and expected hours.

## 8. Confidentiality
MSA may incorporate the NDA or contain equivalent confidentiality obligations. Counsel should reconcile duplication.

## 9. Data Protection
If personal/sensitive data is processed, the DPA may apply. Actual obligations must match the system architecture and vendor chain.

## 10. Security
Do not promise absolute security.
Business intent: reasonable access controls, individual accounts where feasible, MFA where available, least-necessary access, no shared passwords where avoidable, client approval for sensitive AI processing, and documented subprocessors/tools where required. Counsel should define the contractual standard.

## 11. AI-Specific Terms
Counsel should address probabilistic AI output, human review requirements, third-party model providers, client responsibility for consequential decisions, model/vendor changes, no warranty that AI output is error-free, and prohibited/high-risk use cases.

## 12. Intellectual Property
Business intent:
- client receives agreed client-specific deliverables and operating rights
- EchoFrame retains pre-existing tools, templates, methods, generic components, know-how, and reusable materials
- embedded third-party/open-source components remain subject to their licenses

Counsel must define ownership/licensing precisely.

## 13. Acceptance
SOW should contain operational acceptance criteria where relevant. Counsel should define deemed acceptance, review period, and remedy process if needed.
