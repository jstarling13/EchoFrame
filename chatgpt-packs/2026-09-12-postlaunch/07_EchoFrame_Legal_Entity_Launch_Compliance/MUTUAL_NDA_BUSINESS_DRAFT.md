# EchoFrame Mutual NDA — Business Draft for Attorney Review

**NOT FOR SIGNATURE UNTIL REVIEWED BY QUALIFIED COUNSEL.**

## Business intent
Both EchoFrame and the client may disclose confidential information while evaluating or delivering an engagement.

## Counsel should draft/confirm

### Definition of Confidential Information
Should reasonably cover financial information, customer/vendor data, workflows/processes, software/code, credentials/access information, pricing/business plans, proprietary documentation, and technical architecture.

### Exclusions
Typical concepts to evaluate: already public, already lawfully known, independently developed, lawfully received from a third party, required disclosure by law.

### Permitted Use
Use confidential information only to evaluate or perform the business relationship.

### Standard of Care
Counsel to select a reasonable contractual standard consistent with actual operations.

### Representatives
Define when disclosure to employees, contractors, attorneys, CPAs, insurers, cloud vendors, and subprocessors is allowed.

### Compelled Disclosure
Define notice/cooperation requirements where legally permitted.

### Return / Destruction
Align with actual retention obligations, backups, client handoff, and the DPA.

### Term
Counsel to determine agreement term, confidentiality survival period, and treatment of trade secrets.

### No License / Ownership
Disclosure should not transfer IP ownership.

### No Warranty
Information may be provided for evaluation without representation beyond what contract states.

### Governing Law / Venue
OPEN.

### Signatures
Use final legal entity names.
