# EchoFrame Attorney / CPA Review Brief

## Business
EchoFrame is a founder-led AI implementation and workflow automation consulting practice.

## Current commercial model
- $40/hour professional services
- free initial fit conversation
- detailed discovery/diagnostic is paid when required
- travel quoted separately before booking
- $50/day onsite food stipend
- approved third-party tools/API costs may be client-paid or passed through if documented
- no fixed public service packages
- no public checkout

## Delivery model
Typical engagement:
1. fit call
2. written scope
3. paid discovery if needed
4. implementation
5. testing
6. client approval
7. training
8. handoff
9. optional ongoing hourly support

## Typical work
- workflow mapping
- bookkeeping/financial workflow automation support
- process automation
- document routing
- reporting/dashboard work
- customer communication workflows
- AI/tool implementation
- staff training
- systems/access review

## Important service boundaries
EchoFrame does not intend to provide legal, accounting, tax, medical, investment, underwriting, or other licensed professional advice; autonomous high-risk decisions; cybersecurity penetration testing; or 24/7 managed IT.

## Data
Projects may involve ordinary business data, confidential financial data, PII, account-system data, and occasionally PHI-adjacent or regulated environments. High-sensitivity workflows should be gated until architecture and agreements are appropriate.

## Ownership principle
Business intent:
- client should be able to operate the delivered workflow
- client receives agreed client-specific deliverables
- EchoFrame preserves ownership of pre-existing know-how, reusable templates, generic code/components, methods, and tools where appropriate
- client-specific ownership/licensing language needs counsel review

## Documents needing review
- MSA
- SOW
- NDA
- DPA
- Website Terms
- Privacy Policy
- travel/expense terms
- cancellation/termination terms

## Specific decisions counsel should resolve
- legal entity name and formation state
- governing law
- venue/forum
- liability cap
- consequential-damages waiver
- indemnities
- IP ownership/licensing
- confidentiality duration
- data-security commitments
- subprocessors
- breach/incident obligations
- termination rights
- late payment / collection language
- warranty disclaimers
- acceptance mechanics
- client responsibility for business/professional decisions

## Specific decisions CPA should resolve
- entity tax treatment
- state/local registrations
- sales/use-tax treatment of consulting vs automation/software components
- travel reimbursements / pass-through expenses
- estimated-tax obligations
- bookkeeping method
- whether/when S-corporation modeling is appropriate

## Insurance review
Ask a broker/counsel whether the work supports professional liability / E&O, cyber liability, general liability, hired/non-owned auto or travel-related coverage, and client-required COIs.
