# EchoFrame Data Processing Addendum — Business Draft for Attorney Review

**NOT FOR USE UNTIL REVIEWED BY QUALIFIED PRIVACY COUNSEL AND MATCHED TO ACTUAL ARCHITECTURE.**

## Purpose
Use only when EchoFrame processes personal data on behalf of a client in a way that requires a DPA or the client contractually requests one. Do not attach a DPA mechanically to every small engagement without understanding roles and data.

## 1. Parties / Roles
Counsel must determine controller/business role, processor/service-provider role, and any independent-controller activity.

## 2. Processing Details
Each applicable engagement should identify subject matter, duration, purpose, data subjects, categories of personal data, and sensitive data if any.

## 3. Client Instructions
Processing should be limited to documented client instructions and the agreed SOW, subject to law.

## 4. Confidentiality
Authorized persons with access should be bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations.

## 5. Security
Contractual commitments must reflect actual practices. Potential baseline: MFA where available, least privilege, individual accounts, encryption provided by relevant platforms, access removal after engagement, vendor/subprocessor review, incident response process, logging where available and material. Counsel/security reviewer should decide what becomes a contractual warranty.

## 6. Subprocessors
Create/maintain an actual list when applicable: cloud hosting, AI model provider, email provider, automation platform, database, analytics, other vendors. Define authorization model, notice of changes, and objection rights if applicable.

## 7. AI Model Providers
For any AI service receiving client personal data: identify provider, verify account/data-control settings, review retention/training terms, and document approved purpose. Do not promise data is never used for training unless verified for the actual plan/provider/configuration.

## 8. Data Subject Requests
Define reasonable assistance obligations if applicable.

## 9. Incident / Breach
Counsel should specify what qualifies, notification timing, required information, cooperation, and who communicates externally.

## 10. Return / Deletion
Align with client instructions, legal retention, backups, project records, and technical feasibility.

## 11. Cross-Border Transfers
Only include mechanisms that actually apply.

## 12. Audits / Information Rights
Set obligations proportionate to the size/risk of the engagement.

## 13. Liability
Reconcile with MSA.

## 14. Governing Law
Reconcile with MSA and applicable privacy law.

## Required Before Use
- actual data map
- actual vendor list
- counsel review
- actual security controls verified
