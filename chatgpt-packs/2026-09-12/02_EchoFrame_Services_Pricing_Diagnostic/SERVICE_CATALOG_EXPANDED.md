# EchoFrame Expanded Service Catalog

## How to Use This
This is an internal capability catalog, not a public product list.

The website should continue to present a smaller number of service families. During discovery, EchoFrame can select individual capabilities from this catalog and write them into an hourly scope.

# A. Discovery & Workflow Strategy

### Workflow Discovery & Opportunity Mapping
Map one or more current workflows, identify friction, estimate value, and prioritize improvement opportunities.

### AI Readiness Review
Assess where AI is useful, where simple automation is better, and where no automation should be attempted.

### Process Documentation
Turn undocumented work into clear current-state maps, responsibilities, inputs, outputs, controls, and exceptions.

### Automation Feasibility Review
Evaluate whether a proposed workflow can be automated reliably with current systems, APIs, permissions, and data quality.

### Tool / Model Selection
Compare practical tools for a defined workflow. Recommendation should be tied to task requirements rather than model hype.

# B. Financial & Bookkeeping Workflow Automation

### Deposit Matching
Match incoming deposits or remittance information to business records and surface exceptions for review.

### Bank / Card Reconciliation Support
Prepare matching suggestions, exception queues, and supporting documentation while preserving professional review.

### Bill Intake & Approval Routing
Capture bills, classify routing information, send for approval, and organize status.

### Payroll Entry Preparation
Prepare structured payroll-related inputs or summaries for review. Do not make autonomous payroll decisions.

### Transaction Categorization Assistance
Generate suggestions and exception queues subject to bookkeeper/CPA approval.

### Document / Receipt Intake
Organize incoming records, extract fields, validate required information, and route incomplete items.

### Month-End Close Support
Create checklists, status views, exception queues, and document collection workflows.

### Client Request Lists
Automate recurring document requests and track outstanding items.

### Multi-Entity Reporting Support
Maintain entity-level separation while providing owners with consolidated visibility.

### Intercompany Tracking
Track intercompany items and exceptions without making accounting treatment decisions.

# C. Operations Automation

### Email-to-Workflow Automation
Convert structured inbound email into tasks, records, alerts, or routing steps.

### Form & Intake Automation
Create structured intake flows that reduce manual re-entry.

### Approval Workflows
Route requests to the correct person, record approvals, and create an audit trail.

### Spreadsheet Workflow Replacement
Replace fragile repeated spreadsheet steps with more controlled automation where justified.

### File Naming / Organization Automation
Apply consistent naming, routing, storage, and retention logic.

### Recurring Report Generation
Collect inputs, generate recurring reports, and flag anomalies for human review.

### Task / Handoff Automation
Create tasks when defined events occur and route work between teams.

### Exception Queue Design
Instead of automating every edge case, automate the routine path and create a clean queue for human exceptions.

### SOP-to-Workflow Conversion
Translate an existing SOP into a practical automated workflow with human checkpoints.

# D. Customer & Revenue Operations

### Lead Intake
Capture, normalize, enrich, and route inbound leads.

### Lead Follow-Up
Draft or trigger follow-up based on defined rules and human-approved templates.

### Appointment / Scheduling Support
Coordinate scheduling and reminders through approved systems.

### Estimate / Quote Intake
Organize the information required to prepare quotes and flag missing inputs.

### Customer Request Classification
Route inbound requests to the right person or workflow.

### FAQ / Knowledge Assistant
Build controlled retrieval over approved business information.

### Client Status Updates
Generate structured status updates from approved source systems.

### Review / Feedback Collection
Trigger and organize post-service feedback requests.

# E. Reporting & Management Visibility

### Owner Dashboard
Create a compact operational view from existing data sources.

### KPI Reporting
Automate recurring KPI collection and presentation.

### Exception Reporting
Show only what requires attention rather than forcing the owner to review every record.

### Multi-Entity Control View
Present entity-separated status in one management interface.

### Workload / Backlog Visibility
Expose where work is accumulating and how long it has been waiting.

### Lead / Pipeline Reporting
Create simple pipeline visibility from existing CRM or intake systems.

# F. Internal Knowledge & AI Assistance

### Internal Knowledge Search
Build retrieval over approved policies, SOPs, service information, or other internal sources.

### Drafting Assistants
Create controlled assistants for internal drafts that require human review.

### Meeting / Action Capture
Structure notes, decisions, tasks, and follow-ups from meetings.

### Research Workflow
Standardize internal research collection, synthesis, and citation.

### Document Comparison
Compare versions, identify differences, and surface review items.

# G. Training & Adoption

### Owner AI Training
Help the owner understand realistic capabilities, risks, costs, and governance.

### Manager Training
Train process owners to supervise AI-assisted workflows.

### Employee Training
Teach staff how to use the implemented systems safely and consistently.

### Prompt / Workflow Training
Create examples and usage guidance specific to the client's own process.

### Adoption Sessions
Observe actual use after launch and correct confusion, workarounds, or missed steps.

# H. Systems, Access & Governance

### Systems Inventory
Document tools, owners, integrations, and critical data paths.

### Access Review
Review user access, shared credentials, and role separation.

### MFA / Account Hygiene Review
Identify preventable access risks.

### AI Tool Usage Review
Document which AI tools staff currently use and for what data.

### Data Classification Review
Separate ordinary business data from sensitive categories requiring stronger controls.

### Vendor / Tool Review
Evaluate whether a tool's terms and security posture fit the intended workflow.

### Logging / Auditability Design
Ensure important automated actions can be reviewed later.

### Human Approval Checkpoints
Define which decisions must remain human-controlled.

# I. Ongoing AI Consulting

### Automation Maintenance
Fix broken integrations and adjust workflows as systems change.

### New Use-Case Evaluation
Evaluate new workflow opportunities without automatically building them.

### Model / Tool Refresh
Reassess whether a newer tool materially improves a live workflow.

### Quarterly Workflow Review
Review implemented workflows for usage, results, exceptions, and unnecessary complexity.

### Staff Office Hours
Provide scheduled support/training rather than ad hoc unlimited support.

## Services EchoFrame Should Not Market as Core Without Additional Capability
- custom enterprise software development
- 24/7 managed IT
- cybersecurity penetration testing
- autonomous accounting judgment
- legal advice automation
- clinical decision support
- lending decisions
- investment recommendation engines
- autonomous hiring/screening
- safety-critical systems
