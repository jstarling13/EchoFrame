# EchoFrame Sales Pipeline

## Stage 1 — Referred / Identified
Required fields:
- source
- referrer
- company
- contact
- high-level problem
- date introduced

## Stage 2 — Fit Call Scheduled
Exit criteria:
- meeting booked
- basic context captured

## Stage 3 — Qualified
Exit criteria:
- real workflow pain
- sponsor identified
- likely feasible
- next step agreed

## Stage 4 — Paid Discovery or Scope Drafting
Path A: paid discovery needed  
Path B: simple implementation can be scoped directly

## Stage 5 — Proposal / Scope Sent
Track:
- date sent
- expected hours/range
- travel
- dependencies
- follow-up date

## Stage 6 — Verbal Yes / Contracting
Track:
- legal docs
- access/security requirements
- schedule
- initial deposit if ever adopted later

## Stage 7 — Active
Track:
- scope
- hours
- risks
- client owner
- success measures

## Stage 8 — Delivered
Required:
- handoff
- documentation
- training
- final invoice
- measured outcome if available

## Stage 9 — Expansion / Ongoing
Options:
- another workflow
- ongoing hourly support
- training
- quarterly review

## Stage 10 — Closed / No Fit
Reason codes:
- timing
- budget
- no sponsor
- existing tool better
- risk/compliance
- no measurable value
- scope too large
- chose competitor
- unresponsive
