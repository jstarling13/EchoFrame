# EchoFrame Discovery Call Script

## Objective
Determine fit, understand the workflow at a business level, and decide the next paid step.

Recommended length: 20–30 minutes.

## Opening
"Before we talk about tools, I want to understand the process as it works today. If there isn't a practical reason to change it, I don't want to force AI into it."

## Section 1 — Business Context
1. What does the business do?
2. How many locations/entities/teams are involved?
3. Who owns this process today?
4. What changed that made this a priority now?

## Section 2 — Current Workflow
5. Walk me through the process from start to finish.
6. What starts the workflow?
7. Which systems are involved?
8. Where is information re-entered manually?
9. Where does work tend to wait?
10. What requires judgment versus simple rules?
11. What are the common exceptions?

## Section 3 — Cost / Friction
12. How often does this happen?
13. Roughly how much time does it take?
14. Who spends the time?
15. What happens when the process falls behind?
16. Are errors common? What do they create?
17. Have you added staff or outside support because of this workflow?

## Section 4 — Prior Attempts
18. Have you tried to automate this before?
19. What worked?
20. What failed?
21. Is there software you already pay for that should be doing more?

## Section 5 — Access / Risk
22. Does the workflow involve sensitive financial, personal, health, legal, or employee data?
23. Who can approve system access?
24. Are there any actions that must always remain human-approved?
25. Is there a bookkeeper, CPA, attorney, IT provider, or other professional whose process we need to preserve?

## Section 6 — Fit
26. If this were improved, what would a successful result look like?
27. Who needs to approve the work?
28. Is there a deadline or event driving timing?
29. Are you looking for a one-time fix, ongoing help, or not sure yet?

## Close Paths

### Simple / Clear Scope
"I have enough to define a first block of work. I'll send a written scope with the objective, expected hours or range, what I need from you, and the commercial terms."

### Complex / Unclear Scope
"The first paid step should be discovery and mapping. I'll bill that at the normal hourly rate, document the workflow, and come back with a prioritized implementation plan before we build anything substantial."

### Poor Fit
"I don't think custom AI work is the right first answer here. The better option is [existing tool / process clarification / professional review]."

## Do Not Do on Free Call
- detailed system architecture
- custom implementation design
- unpaid workflow mapping
- writing specifications
- troubleshooting the client's systems live
