# EchoFrame Referral Partner Playbook

## Primary Partner Types
- bookkeepers
- CPAs
- fractional CFOs/controllers
- business attorneys for operational referrals, not legal automation
- managed IT providers that do not offer workflow consulting
- business consultants
- local professional-service advisors

## Partner Value Proposition
EchoFrame helps solve the workflow problems that trusted advisors can see but do not want to implement themselves.

For bookkeepers and CPAs:
- remove repetitive preparation work
- improve client document intake
- improve exception handling
- make multi-entity reporting easier
- give the professional more capacity
- preserve accounting judgment and review

## Positioning Line
**EchoFrame handles the workflow and automation work around the accounting process so the bookkeeper or CPA can stay focused on the work that actually requires professional judgment.**

## What EchoFrame Is Not Asking the Partner to Do
- sell AI
- guarantee savings
- scope the implementation
- provide technical support
- put their reputation behind a system they have not reviewed

The partner only needs to identify clients with visible workflow friction and make an introduction.

## Ideal Referral Signals
A partner hears:
- "We do all of this by hand."
- "We can't keep up with the paperwork."
- "We have five entities and no clean owner view."
- "I spend hours matching deposits."
- "Everything is in spreadsheets."
- "No one follows up fast enough."
- "Only one employee knows this process."
- "We need another admin person."

## Intro Format
Simple three-party introduction:
1. Why the partner thought of EchoFrame
2. One sentence on the workflow issue
3. Jacob takes over discovery

## Partner Follow-Up
After an engagement:
- thank the referrer
- confirm the engagement occurred, subject to client confidentiality
- ask whether the client experience met expectations
- do not disclose private financial/operational details without permission
- make it easy for the partner to introduce another client

## Partner One-Pager Copy

### Headline
**When the Process Is the Problem, Not the People**

### Body
EchoFrame helps small businesses reduce repetitive administrative work with practical AI and workflow automation.

Typical projects involve bookkeeping support, document intake, reconciliation workflows, multi-entity tracking, reporting, customer follow-up, and other processes that currently depend on manual copying, spreadsheets, or inbox management.

EchoFrame is not a replacement for the bookkeeper, CPA, or staff member who owns the professional judgment. The goal is to give those people better systems and more capacity.

Projects are scoped directly with the business and billed at $40/hour, with travel quoted separately when onsite work is needed.

### CTA
If a client has a workflow everyone knows is too manual, an introduction is enough.
