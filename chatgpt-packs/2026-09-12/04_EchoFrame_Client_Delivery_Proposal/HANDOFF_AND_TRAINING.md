# EchoFrame Handoff & Training Standard

## Every Completed Build Should Include
- what the workflow does
- what triggers it
- which systems it touches
- who owns it
- required credentials/accounts
- normal output
- exceptions
- human approval points
- known limitations
- vendor/API dependencies
- how to pause or disable it
- how to get support

## Training Session
Cover:
1. normal use
2. what not to do
3. how to recognize failure
4. how to handle exceptions
5. who can change settings
6. when EchoFrame should be contacted

## Ownership Goal
The client should not need EchoFrame for routine operation unless ongoing support was intentionally purchased.

## Follow-Up
After initial use:
- collect user issues
- revise SOP
- remove unnecessary complexity
- document confirmed business impact
