# EchoFrame Client Onboarding Checklist

## Commercial
- proposal approved
- agreement/SOW signed
- billing contact confirmed
- travel approved
- expected hour range acknowledged

## People
- executive sponsor
- process owner
- technical/system contact
- bookkeeper/CPA/other professional reviewer if relevant
- users for testing
- approver for launch

## Workflow
- current process documented
- start/end points
- volume/frequency
- exceptions
- required approvals
- success measure

## Systems
- system list
- account owner
- access method
- API/export capability
- sandbox/test availability
- MFA status
- shared-password issues identified
- required vendor permissions

## Data
- source of truth
- data sensitivity
- PII/PHI/financial classification
- retention needs
- sample/test data
- client approval for any AI processing

## Risk
- actions that must remain human-approved
- payment authority
- accounting treatment
- clinical/legal/employment/investment decisions
- audit/logging requirements

## Delivery
- kickoff scheduled
- test users identified
- communication channel
- change-control owner
- launch window
- training requirement
- handoff expectation
