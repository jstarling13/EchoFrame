# EchoFrame FAQ Copy

## What does EchoFrame actually do?
EchoFrame maps repetitive business workflows and implements practical AI or automation to reduce manual work, improve visibility, or make the process more reliable.

## What kinds of businesses are a good fit?
The strongest fit is an owner-led or closely managed business with a recurring process that is obviously too manual, too dependent on one person, or spread across disconnected systems.

## Do you only work with bookkeeping and accounting workflows?
No. Financial workflows are a strong specialty, but EchoFrame also works on operations, customer follow-up, reporting, intake, approvals, internal knowledge, and staff enablement.

## Are you trying to replace bookkeepers or CPAs?
No. EchoFrame is positioned to improve the systems around their work so repetitive steps take less time while professional judgment and review stay with the professional.

## Do you charge by the project?
The current model is $40/hour for professional services. Travel is quoted separately, and onsite work includes a $50/day food stipend.

## Why hourly instead of fixed packages?
Because workflow projects vary materially by the systems, data, exceptions, number of entities, and level of client involvement. EchoFrame still defines scope and expected effort so hourly billing does not mean open-ended work.

## Is the first call free?
Recommended answer if approved:
Yes. The initial fit conversation is free and brief. Detailed discovery, workflow mapping, technical design, and implementation are paid work.

## Do you work onsite?
Yes, when it materially improves the project. Travel is quoted separately before booking.

## Will I need to replace my existing software?
Usually not. EchoFrame prefers to improve the workflow around existing systems when they are adequate. If a proven tool already solves the problem better than custom work, that will be said directly.

## Who owns what you build?
The commercial goal is for the client to be able to operate and extend the delivered system. Formal ownership terms should match the written agreement.

## Can you work with sensitive data?
Only when the data, vendor terms, permissions, and security requirements can be handled responsibly. Sensitive workflows may require additional review or may be declined.

## Can AI make decisions automatically?
Some low-risk routine steps can be automated. High-impact decisions and professional judgments should retain appropriate human review.

## Do you guarantee savings?
No. EchoFrame can estimate value using current workflow data and measure outcomes, but it does not guarantee savings.

## What happens after launch?
The project includes documentation and handoff appropriate to the scope. Additional support can continue on an hourly basis if needed.
