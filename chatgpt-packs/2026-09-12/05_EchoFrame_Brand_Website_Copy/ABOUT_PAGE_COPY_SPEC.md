## Main Narrative
EchoFrame is a founder-led AI implementation and workflow automation practice built around a simple premise: most businesses do not need more AI ideas. They need someone to understand how the work actually moves, identify what is worth changing, and implement it without creating another system the team cannot maintain.

Jacob Starling works directly with clients on workflow discovery, implementation, testing, training, and handoff.

His background in finance informs the way EchoFrame approaches automation: start with the economics of the workflow, preserve controls, measure the result, and do not automate judgment simply because a model can produce an answer.

## Direct Delivery
### Headline
**One Person Accountable for the Work**

Copy:
EchoFrame does not use a layered account model. The person scoping the workflow is also involved in building and transferring the result.

That makes the practice intentionally boutique. It also makes responsibility clear.

## AI Philosophy
### Headline
**Use AI Where It Earns Its Place**

Copy:
Some problems need AI. Some need ordinary automation. Some need a better process. Some are already solved by software the client owns.

EchoFrame is designed to make that distinction before adding complexity.

## Bookkeeper / Finance Angle
### Headline
**Automation Around Professional Judgment**

Copy:
Financial and bookkeeping workflows are a particular focus. The goal is not to automate the professional out of the process. It is to reduce the repetitive work around matching, intake, routing, reporting, and exception handling so the professional can focus on the work that requires judgment.
