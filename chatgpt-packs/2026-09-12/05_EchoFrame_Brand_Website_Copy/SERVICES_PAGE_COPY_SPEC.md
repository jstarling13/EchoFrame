# Services Page Copy Specification

## Hero
### Headline
**Practical AI and Automation, Scoped to the Work**

### Intro
EchoFrame works across financial, operational, customer, reporting, and internal workflows. The service list is deliberately broad because the starting point is the process, not a prepackaged product.

## Service 1
### Workflow Discovery & Opportunity Mapping
Understand the current process, identify friction, estimate value, and decide what is worth improving before building.

## Service 2
### Bookkeeping & Financial Workflow Automation
Reduce repetitive preparation, matching, intake, routing, and reconciliation work while preserving the bookkeeper or CPA's review and judgment.

Examples:
- deposit matching
- reconciliation support
- bill intake
- payroll-entry preparation
- month-end workflow support
- document collection

## Service 3
### Multi-Entity & Intercompany Tracking
Create clearer visibility across multiple entities without collapsing the separation required for accounting and operational control.

## Service 4
### Process & Workflow Automation
Replace repetitive copying, spreadsheet steps, email routing, approvals, and manual handoffs with a more controlled workflow.

## Service 5
### Customer Communication & Lead Follow-Up
Improve intake, scheduling, routing, reminders, and follow-up while keeping the business in control of customer-facing decisions.

## Service 6
### Reporting & Owner Dashboards
Turn existing system data into recurring management views, KPI reporting, backlog visibility, and exception reporting.

## Service 7
### Staff Training
Train the people who will actually use and own the system, including safe AI use, workflow-specific instructions, and exception handling.

## Service 8
### Systems & Security Review
Review access, tool usage, sensitive data paths, approval points, and practical controls before or alongside implementation.

## Service 9
### Ongoing AI Consulting
Use hourly support for troubleshooting, new use-case evaluation, workflow improvement, model/tool changes, and periodic review.
