# New York Outreach and Introduction Scripts

**Purpose:** Make outreach specific and referral-friendly

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Partner outreach

I help established businesses convert repetitive document, email, and spreadsheet work into tested AI workflows, then train the team to own them. Your clients may be experimenting without a process or data framework. Could we compare notes for 20 minutes on the patterns you are seeing?

## Owner outreach

[Mutual contact] suggested I reach out. I work with established businesses on one practical problem at a time: mapping a repeated workflow, implementing the right AI/automation support, testing it, and training the people who own it. I noticed [specific operational signal]. Is [workflow hypothesis] a real issue, or am I off?

## Onsite meeting framework

15 minutes context; 30 minutes live workflow walk-through; 20 minutes bottleneck/data/risk mapping; 10 minutes opportunity screen; 10 minutes next-step decision. Do not accept sensitive documents before data instructions and authorization.

## Trip batching rule

Book a trip only with three confirmed qualified meetings, one paid onsite day, or expected gross profit at least three times incremental travel cost. Group by transit radius; preserve remote alternatives; track travel time separately from reimbursable expense.
