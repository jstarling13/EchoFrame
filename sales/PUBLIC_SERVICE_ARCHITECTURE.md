# EchoFrame Public Service Architecture

## Goal
Keep the website simple while retaining a broad internal capability set.

## Recommended Public Families

### 1. Find the Right Work to Automate
Includes:
- workflow discovery
- opportunity mapping
- readiness review
- process documentation
- feasibility review

Public outcome:
**Know where automation is worth doing before spending money building it.**

### 2. Financial & Bookkeeping Workflow Automation
Includes:
- deposit matching
- reconciliation support
- bill intake
- document intake
- payroll preparation
- multi-entity tracking
- month-end workflow support

Public outcome:
**Reduce repetitive financial administration while preserving professional review.**

### 3. Process & Workflow Automation
Includes:
- forms
- email routing
- approvals
- spreadsheets
- recurring reports
- handoffs
- exception queues

Public outcome:
**Turn repetitive manual processes into reliable workflows.**

### 4. Customer Communication & Follow-Up
Includes:
- lead intake
- scheduling
- follow-up
- request routing
- status communication

Public outcome:
**Respond faster and more consistently without losing human control.**

### 5. Reporting & Owner Visibility
Includes:
- dashboards
- KPI reporting
- backlog visibility
- exception reporting
- multi-entity views

Public outcome:
**See what requires attention without assembling the answer manually.**

### 6. Staff Training & AI Enablement
Includes:
- owner/manager/employee training
- workflow-specific guidance
- adoption review

Public outcome:
**Make the system usable by the people who actually run the business.**

### 7. Systems, Access & AI Governance
Includes:
- system inventory
- access review
- tool review
- data classification
- approval checkpoints

Public outcome:
**Use AI and automation with clearer ownership and controls.**

### 8. Ongoing AI Consulting
Includes:
- maintenance
- optimization
- new-use-case review
- tool/model refresh
- office hours

Public outcome:
**Keep useful systems current without turning EchoFrame into permanent vendor lock-in.**

## Website Rule
Do not expose every internal micro-service as its own card.
The public site should sell outcomes and capability families.
Detailed scope belongs in proposals.
