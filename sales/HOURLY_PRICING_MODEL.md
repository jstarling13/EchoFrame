# Hourly Pricing Model (current)

**Purpose:** The live pricing model as of 2026-09-12. Supersedes the fixed
O1-O5 packages in `OFFER_CATALOG.md` for new client work — that file is
kept as-is for historical reference, not deleted.

## Rate card

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Professional time | $40/hour, billed for actual hours worked |
| Travel fee | Flat fee quoted per trip to cover mileage/airfare and lodging. A typical single-day regional trip runs around $1,250; confirmed before booking. |
| Onsite food stipend | $50/day for any day worked onsite at a client location |

No fixed packages. No public checkout. Every project gets a written quote
after a discovery conversation, scoped to realistic hours.

## What's advertised (service capabilities, not priced SKUs)

- Workflow Discovery & Opportunity Mapping
- Bookkeeping & Financial Workflow Automation (deposit matching, bank/card
  reconciliation, bill intake & approval routing, payroll entry prep)
- Multi-Entity & Intercompany Tracking
- Process & Workflow Automation
- Customer Communication & Lead Follow-Up
- Reporting & Owner Dashboards
- Staff Training
- Systems & Security Review
- Ongoing AI Consulting (month to month, hourly)

Source of truth for the live website: `website/lib/services.ts`.

## Why this changed

Fixed packages ($2,500-$45,000) implied a menu of productized SKUs.
Real client work (see `Plan for Dr. Hendizadeh.pdf`) is scoped and quoted
individually. One simple hourly rate is easier to sell, easier to explain,
and matches how work is actually billed today.
