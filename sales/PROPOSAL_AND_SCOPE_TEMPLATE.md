# Proposal and Scope Template

**Purpose:** Convert discovery into an enforceable, measurable offer

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Executive summary

State the observed workflow, business consequence, proposed future state, why now, and the smallest reasonable scope.

## Scope table

For each workflow: owner, trigger, inputs, systems, model/automation role, human review, outputs, data class, volume, acceptance criteria, fallback, exclusions.

## Plan

Discover/map; design/security gate; build; acceptance test; training; launch; stabilization; handoff.

## Success criteria

Include quality threshold, cycle-time target, adoption target, exception rate, user competency, documentation/admin transfer, and measurement method. Avoid guaranteed business outcomes.

## Dependencies

Client access, timely approvals, test data, process owner, subject-matter reviewers, vendor accounts, security/legal approval, and stable source systems.

## Change control

Any change to workflows, systems, data classes, users, integrations, deliverables, or acceptance criteria is assessed for schedule, fee, and risk. Work begins only after written approval.
