## Workflow Opportunity Diagnostic

- **Client:** Up to 3 departments, 8 interviews, 12 workflows inventoried
- **Problem:** Bottlenecked, inconsistent, or ungoverned knowledge work appropriate to this scope.
- **Deliverables:** Readiness score, ranked opportunity register, one current-state map, 90-day roadmap
- **Duration:** 10 business days
- **Meetings:** Kickoff, two interviews, findings review
- **Implementation:** No production implementation; one proof-of-concept outline
- **Training:** 60-minute owner briefing
- **Support:** 7 days of email clarification
- **Exclusions:** Software licenses, production integrations, regulated decision automation
- **Price:** $2,500
- **Deposit:** $2,500
- **Upgrade:** Credit $1,000 toward O2 or O3 if signed within 30 days

## Workflow Build Sprint

- **Client:** One department; up to 2 medium-complexity workflows and 10 users
- **Problem:** Bottlenecked, inconsistent, or ungoverned knowledge work appropriate to this scope.
- **Deliverables:** Workflow designs, configured solution, test evidence, SOPs, prompt library, admin handoff
- **Duration:** 4 weeks
- **Meetings:** Kickoff, weekly build reviews, training, launch review
- **Implementation:** Included for approved tools and client-owned accounts
- **Training:** Two 90-minute sessions
- **Support:** 14 days post-launch
- **Exclusions:** Custom enterprise software, migrations, 24/7 support, regulated autonomous decisions
- **Price:** $7,500
- **Deposit:** $3,750
- **Upgrade:** Apply reusable assets toward O3; transition to O5

## AI Operations Transformation

- **Client:** Up to 3 departments, 5 workflows, 30 users
- **Problem:** Bottlenecked, inconsistent, or ungoverned knowledge work appropriate to this scope.
- **Deliverables:** Blueprint, implementation plan, implemented workflows, governance pack, training, ROI baseline, final handoff
- **Duration:** 8-10 weeks
- **Meetings:** Kickoff, weekly steering, department workshops, launch, executive review
- **Implementation:** Included within agreed systems and complexity budget
- **Training:** Owner, manager, employee tracks; four sessions total
- **Support:** 30 days post-launch
- **Exclusions:** ERP replacement, unscoped data cleanup, custom mobile apps, legal compliance certification
- **Price:** $18,000
- **Deposit:** $7,200
- **Upgrade:** O5 support or O4 expansion

## Enterprise AI Operations Program

- **Client:** Up to 6 departments, 12 workflows, 100 users; custom scope above this level
- **Problem:** Bottlenecked, inconsistent, or ungoverned knowledge work appropriate to this scope.
- **Deliverables:** Portfolio roadmap, governance, implementations, change management, train-the-trainer, ROI dashboard
- **Duration:** 16-20 weeks
- **Meetings:** Executive steering biweekly; workstream sessions weekly
- **Implementation:** Phased implementation with security and acceptance gates
- **Training:** Role-based plus internal champion certification
- **Support:** 60 days post-launch
- **Exclusions:** Third-party license fees, extensive data engineering, formal legal/security attestations
- **Price:** $45,000
- **Deposit:** $13,500
- **Upgrade:** O5 Enterprise support; separately scoped expansion

## Workflow Assurance & Enablement

- **Client:** Up to 8 support hours, 2 minor workflow changes, monitoring review
- **Problem:** Bottlenecked, inconsistent, or ungoverned knowledge work appropriate to this scope.
- **Deliverables:** Health report, issue log, improvements, office hours, quarterly value review
- **Duration:** Monthly; 3-month initial term
- **Meetings:** Monthly operations review plus office hours
- **Implementation:** Minor changes only; major work quoted separately
- **Training:** Monthly office hours
- **Support:** Business-hours response: 1 business day critical, 2 standard
- **Exclusions:** Net-new systems, 24/7 monitoring, unlimited revisions
- **Price:** $2,500
- **Deposit:** $2,500
- **Upgrade:** Additional 10-hour block: $2,000; enterprise retainer from $5,000/month

## Payment schedules

O1: 100% before kickoff. O2: 50% before kickoff, 50% before production launch. O3: 40% before kickoff, 30% after approved blueprint, 30% before launch. O4: 30% kickoff, 25% blueprint approval, 25% implementation acceptance, 20% before final handoff. O5: monthly in advance; 3-month initial term.

## Add-ons

Additional workflow: from $2,500. Additional onsite day: $2,000 plus approved travel. Additional training cohort: $1,250. Data cleanup/migration: scoped separately. Ten-hour support block: $2,000. Security/compliance specialist: pass-through or separately quoted.
