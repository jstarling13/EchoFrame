# Discovery Script and Questionnaire

**Purpose:** Diagnose before prescribing

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Opening

“I want to understand the work, the consequence, and what a safe useful result would look like. I will not recommend a tool until we know those.” Confirm 30 minutes, decision participants, and note-taking permission.

## Workflow questions

What repeats weekly? Where does work wait? What is re-keyed? Which deliverables require senior review? Where do errors or inconsistency appear? What has already been tried? Who owns the process? What systems and documents are involved? What happens when the process fails?

## ROI questions

Monthly volume; minutes per case by role; loaded hourly cost; overtime/contractor spend; error/rework rate; backlog; response time; conversion/value per opportunity; current tool spend; expected adoption; implementation effort. Ask what expenditure would actually decline versus what capacity would be redeployed.

## Risk questions

Data classes; contractual restrictions; PHI/PII/financial/legal content; external commitments; human approver; audit/log needs; access roles; retention/deletion; acceptable fallback; prohibited outputs.

## Close

Restate problem, consequence, constraints, and desired outcome. Recommend no fit, a paid diagnostic, or a scoped build. Agree on one next action and date.
