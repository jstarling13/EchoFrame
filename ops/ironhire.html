How it works

Your time starts at the interview. Not the job post.

Crew Hire handles everything before the face-to-face so you're only spending time on candidates worth hiring.

1

Tell us who you're looking for

Describe the role, the skills you need, the pay range, and any deal-breakers. Crew Hire writes the job posting, lists it across the major trade job boards, and starts pulling in applicants.

2

We screen and filter the applicants

Every applicant goes through a pre-screen — certifications, experience, availability, reliability history. The ones who don't meet your criteria get filtered out before they reach your inbox.

3

Qualified candidates land on your calendar

The candidates who pass screening get scheduled for an interview — confirmed, reminded, and ready to show up. You get a shortlist, not a pile of resumes.

What's included

A full hiring pipeline without an HR department.

Multi-board job posting

Filtering

Pre-screening & qualification

Automated pre-screens check for the skills, certifications, and experience you actually need — so your shortlist is made up of people who can do the job, not just people who applied.

Scheduling

Confirmed interview booking

Qualified candidates book directly into your calendar. Automated reminders go out before the interview. No-show rates drop because the commitment is made before they forget about it.

Pipeline
