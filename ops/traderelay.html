How it works

Call Router sits in front of your existing phone number — no new number to give out, no change to how customers reach you. Setup takes under 30 minutes.

Every call gets answered and qualified

Call Router greets the caller, gathers the job details — location, issue type, urgency — and determines whether it's a same-day call or something that can be scheduled.
