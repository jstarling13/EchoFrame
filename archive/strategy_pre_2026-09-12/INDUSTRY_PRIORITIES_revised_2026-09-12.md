# EchoFrame Industry Priorities — Revised

## Principle
Prioritize workflow patterns before industry labels.

EchoFrame should pursue industries where:
- repetitive administrative work is common
- buyers can act quickly
- existing software exposes usable APIs / exports
- implementation can be measured
- the workflow does not require unsupported regulatory infrastructure

## Wave 1 — Immediate

### 1. Bookkeeping / Accounting-Adjacent Small Businesses
Why:
- strongest referral channel
- clear repetitive workflows
- direct founder proof / experience
- strong assist-not-replace positioning

Use cases:
- deposit matching
- reconciliation support
- bill intake
- document routing
- exception lists
- owner reporting
- intercompany tracking

### 2. Professional Practices
Examples:
- medical practice administrative workflows excluding clinical decisions
- legal-office administration excluding legal advice generation
- consulting/professional services
- multi-location practices

Use cases:
- intake
- reporting
- document organization
- scheduling
- non-clinical follow-up
- financial workflow support

### 3. Multi-Entity Owner / Operator Businesses
Why:
- high visibility problem
- strong relevance to current prospect example
- repeated reporting/reconciliation needs

Use cases:
- entity-level dashboards
- intercompany tracking
- exception reporting
- consolidated owner views without collapsing legal/accounting separation

### 4. Commercial Real Estate / Property Administration
Use cases:
- tenant workflow
- remittance intake
- owner reporting
- document routing
- recurring communication
- maintenance/admin workflow

## Wave 2 — After Proof

### 5. Specialty Contractors / Construction Administration
Use cases:
- estimate intake
- job documentation
- invoice routing
- status reporting
- lead follow-up

### 6. Recruiting / Staffing
Use cases:
- intake
- communication
- scheduling
- reporting
- administrative coordination
Avoid autonomous screening or employment decisions without appropriate review.

### 7. Architecture / Engineering / B2B Professional Services
Use cases:
- project administration
- reporting
- document workflows
- proposal support
- internal knowledge retrieval

## Gate / Delay
Do not actively sell unsupported high-risk automation involving:
- clinical decisions
- PHI-heavy AI workflows without approved architecture
- legal advice
- lending/underwriting
- investment recommendations
- autonomous employment decisions
- biometric surveillance
- safety-critical control
- autonomous financial commitments
