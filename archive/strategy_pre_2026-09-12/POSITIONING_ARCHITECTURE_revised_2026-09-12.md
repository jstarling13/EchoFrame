# EchoFrame Positioning Architecture — Revised

## 1. Category
AI implementation and workflow automation consulting.

## 2. Audience
Primary launch audience:
Owner-led and closely managed small businesses with visible administrative friction, especially those introduced by trusted bookkeepers, CPAs, and professional advisors.

Secondary audience:
Established lower-mid-market businesses with a clear executive sponsor and process owner.

## 3. Problem
Businesses accumulate manual work across:
- accounting systems
- email
- spreadsheets
- forms
- document intake
- reporting
- follow-up
- approvals
- multi-entity operations

The work is often tolerated because each individual task looks small. In aggregate, it consumes meaningful labor and creates dependency on a few people.

## 4. Promise
EchoFrame identifies the highest-value workflow problems, implements practical improvements, and transfers the resulting system to the client.

## 5. Mechanism
The OWNED Method:
Observe → Weigh → Navigate → Engineer → Demonstrate and transfer

Internal execution:
Discover → Map → Measure → Prioritize → Design → Build → Test → Train → Transfer → Improve

## 6. Evidence
Use only evidence that can be substantiated:
- measured time reduction
- reduced manual steps
- exception rates
- response-time improvement
- reconciliation throughput
- documented training completion
- client-owned system artifacts

Do not turn estimates into claims.

## 7. Commercial Simplicity
One hourly rate across consulting and implementation:
$40/hour.

Travel and onsite costs are disclosed separately.

No public package matrix.

## 8. Tone
Corporate clarity without pretending to be a large firm.

Use:
- short declarative headlines
- business outcomes
- operational specificity
- calm confidence

Avoid:
- "revolutionary"
- "game-changing"
- "AI-powered everything"
- "10x"
- "replace your team"
- startup-jargon density
- fake plural "we" if Jacob is the actual delivery lead

## 9. Messaging Hierarchy
### Level 1 — What
AI implementation and workflow automation.

### Level 2 — Why
Reduce repetitive work and improve operational visibility.

### Level 3 — How
Built around the client's actual process and existing systems.

### Level 4 — Why EchoFrame
Direct founder involvement, transparent billing, practical implementation, transfer to the client.

## 10. CTA Ladder
1. Talk through one workflow.
2. Map the current process.
3. Scope a first implementation.
4. Prove value.
5. Expand only if the result justifies it.
