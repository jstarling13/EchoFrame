# Naming Framework and Candidates

**Purpose:** Select a durable brand without assuming availability

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Criteria

Credible with traditional owners; broad beyond any vendor; clear pronunciation; distinctive search footprint; available trademark/domain/social; works as “[Name] AI Operations”; avoids “genius,” “magic,” “bot,” and transformation clichés.

## Candidates

1. **Practical AI Operations**: clearest category, weak distinctiveness.
2. **Commonwork Systems**: human and operational, broad.
3. **Fieldwork AI**: grounded and active, may imply field services.
4. **Northline Operations**: credible, geographic connotation.
5. **Plainwork Labs**: approachable, “Labs” slightly experimental.
6. **Method & Machine**: memorable, boutique.
7. **Operator’s AI**: direct, possessive/domain complexity.
8. **Workcraft Systems**: capability and transfer, possible crowded term.

## Weighted evaluation

Score 1-5: credibility 25%, distinctiveness 20%, clarity 20%, extensibility 15%, verbal ease 10%, preliminary availability 10%. Do not perform final selection until counsel completes trademark screening and the owner confirms domain economics.

## Recommendation

Use “Practical AI Operations” only as a working descriptive brand. Shortlist Commonwork Systems, Method & Machine, and Workcraft Systems for clearance.

## Decision (2026-08-29, corrected 2026-08-29)

**White Oak Operations is the selected working company name, pending
trademark, entity-name, domain, and common-law clearance** — not from the
candidate list above (that list and its scoring are preserved here as the
