# Brand Voice and Terminology

**Purpose:** Keep copy credible, concrete, and human

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Voice

Calm, specific, candid, operational, respectful of experienced employees. Use short sentences, real nouns, explicit limitations, and measurable claims.

## Use

workflow, process owner, acceptance test, human review, evidence, operating impact, capacity created, implementation, adoption, knowledge transfer, vendor-neutral, controlled pilot.

## Avoid

revolutionize, unlock, seamless, game-changing, AI-powered everything, replace your staff, guaranteed savings, hallucination-free, fully autonomous, proprietary intelligence unless true.

## Claim rules

Separate observed fact, client estimate, modeled scenario, and forecast. Never call hours “savings” without spend reduction. Never imply a model or workflow is error-free. State where human judgment remains.
