# EchoFrame Brand Voice Guide

> **Reviewed and declined for the live site, 2026-09-12.** Jacob explicitly
> chose to keep the Big-Four/"Tier-1 management consulting" tone already
> implemented on the site (thought-leadership headlines, trademarked
> service names, punchy quantified headlines) over the calmer voice this
> guide recommends. Kept for reference in case direction changes later —
> not currently the active voice.

## Voice
EchoFrame should sound:
- confident
- precise
- grounded
- modern
- commercially literate
- technically capable
- direct

## Voice Formula
**Corporate clarity + founder accountability + practical operating detail**

## Use
- short headlines
- specific verbs
- concrete business problems
- simple explanations
- measured confidence
- first-person founder language where personal accountability matters

## Avoid
- "revolutionize"
- "transform your business with the power of AI"
- "cutting-edge solutions"
- "unlock"
- "10x"
- "game-changing"
- "AI-powered" repeated as filler
- fake enterprise plural
- vague claims like "drive efficiency" without saying how
- promises of guaranteed savings
- "replace staff"

## Headline Style
Prefer:
**Fix the Work That Shouldn't Still Be Manual**

Over:
**Unlock the Future of AI-Powered Business Transformation**

Prefer:
**Automation Built Around the Operation**

Over:
**Innovative Solutions for Tomorrow's Enterprise**

## Tone in Sales Copy
Calm enough to say when AI is not the right solution.

That is part of credibility, not a weakness.
