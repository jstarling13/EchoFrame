What Changed & What We Caught

What changed this month

Revenue was your best of the year — up 11% on the spring AC-repair rush. Eight of those jobs came from the Google Ads campaign, which is now paying for itself roughly 19× over.

Margin widened to 20.8% because revenue grew faster than payroll. You added one tech in March; this is the first full month they were billable rather than just a cost.

The books are caught up. Last month you carried 47 uncategorized transactions. All 47 are now sorted, matched to invoices where possible, and explained above — nothing is sitting in "Ask My Accountant."

Miscategorizations we caught

! An $1,180 Carrier equipment charge was auto-booked as Supplies → moved to COGS — Equipment. Supplies were overstated; gross margin on that job is now correct.
! A $2,400 owner transfer to personal savings was sitting in Expenses → reclassed to Owner's Draw. It's not a business cost and shouldn't reduce your profit.
! A $540 duplicate from a double-swiped Ferguson charge was flagged; the credit was matched, so it nets to $0 instead of inflating Supplies.
The One Thing

Move your van loan payment off the credit card.

Your $720/mo service-van payment is running through the business card and getting auto-split between interest and principal each month — but the card charges 21.9% APR on the carried balance. Paying it from checking instead would stop roughly $1,420 a year in avoidable interest. That's the single cleanup with real dollars attached this month.
