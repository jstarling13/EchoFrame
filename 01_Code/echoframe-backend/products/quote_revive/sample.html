How Quote Revive Works

When a quote you've sent goes quiet, Quote Revive starts a three-touch sequence on its own — a friendly Day 2 text, a Day 7 email with light urgency, and a Day 14 "breakup" that forces a clean yes, no, or later. Replies route straight back to you; quotes that say no are closed so you stop chasing them. Days Cold counts from the date the quote was sent, and the price stays locked through the sequence so the customer never feels penalized for taking their time.
