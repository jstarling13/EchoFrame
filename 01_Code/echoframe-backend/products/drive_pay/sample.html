The One Thing

Turn on pay-before-release for tickets over $500 — it would have pulled ~$5,100 forward this month.

Drive Pay collected $46,180 in June, and 85% of it landed before the car left the lot. But the 13 invoices that weren't paid at pickup averaged six days to collect — and one $1,180 transmission job is now 40 days past due after the customer drove off. Nine of those thirteen were over $500. Switch on "confirm payment before keys" for tickets above $500 and most of that $6,920 collects at the counter instead of aging into receivables — starting with the job that's already in collections.
