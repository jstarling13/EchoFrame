How Clear Ledger Works

Every open invoice is tracked from its due date and moved through the sequence automatically — friendly at day 3, firm at day 14, final notice at day 30 — with messaging tuned to recover the balance while protecting the relationship. Most invoices clear at the friendly or firm stage and never need your attention. When one reaches final notice, goes silent, or comes back with a dispute, Clear Ledger flags it and hands it to you with the full contact history. This cycle, the sequence collected $14,320 and pulled average days-to-pay from 44 down to 31.
