# Business Audit Report

- **Engine:** `business_audit_engine.py`
- **Template:** `business_audit.html.j2`

## Documents the customer provides

Your file should include your business context and recent financials, with:
- Years in business and number of employees
- The last 3 months of revenue and expenses (one row per month)
- Your top 3 local competitors
- The main challenge you're facing right now

**Where to export from:** Pull your monthly revenue and expense totals from your accounting tool (QuickBooks, Xero, Wave), add your context rows, then export to CSV.
