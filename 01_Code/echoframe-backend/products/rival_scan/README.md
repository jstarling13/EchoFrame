# Rival Scan

- **Engine:** `rival_scan_engine.py`
- **Template:** `rival_scan.html.j2`

## Documents the customer provides

Your file should include:
- Your business and the one key item you want compared (e.g. Lg 1-topping pizza)
- Your own price for that item
- 3-5 competitors you want watched
- For each competitor: their price for that same item
- Any promos or review highlights you already know (optional)

**Where to export from:** List your competitors and their advertised prices (from their menus or sites) in a spreadsheet alongside your own price, then export to CSV.
