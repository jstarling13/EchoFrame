# New York Market Entry

**Purpose:** Build a referral-led, travel-disciplined launch

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Geographic sequence

Start with one reachable relationship cluster, not “all New York.” Cluster prospects by NYC/Long Island, Westchester/Hudson Valley, Capital Region, Central New York, and Western New York. Add a cluster only when at least three qualified meetings or one paid engagement justify travel.

## Referral targets

CPAs, business attorneys, commercial bankers, insurance brokers, payroll/HR advisors, MSPs, fractional CFOs/COOs, industry association leaders, commercial real estate brokers, software implementers, and PE/independent sponsor operating contacts.

## Industry sequence

Accounting and advisory operations; property management/CRE; construction/specialty trades; recruiting/staffing; architecture/engineering and B2B services. Use workflow examples without claiming sector expertise not earned.

## Networking targets

Trade associations, chambers with established-business membership, owner peer groups, professional societies, commercial real estate and construction events, accounting/legal technology groups. Choose events based on target attendance and pre-booked meetings, not ticket count.

## Partnership offer

Provide a no-cost 30-minute workflow-risk briefing for the partner’s clients; never pay undisclosed referral compensation; document conflicts and professional rules; refer complementary work back when appropriate.
