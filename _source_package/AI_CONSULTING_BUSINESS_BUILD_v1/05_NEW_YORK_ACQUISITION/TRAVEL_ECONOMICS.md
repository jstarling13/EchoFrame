# Travel Economics

**Purpose:** Approve travel using contribution economics

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Formula

Incremental trip cost = transport + lodging + local transit + meals + event fees + incremental insurance + opportunity cost of delivery time. Expected trip contribution = sum(probability of close × expected project gross profit) + paid onsite fees − incremental trip cost.

## Gate

Approve if expected contribution is positive, expected gross profit is at least 3× cash travel cost, and travel does not endanger committed delivery. Strategic exceptions require a written reason and capped budget.

## Client terms

Travel is remote-first. Onsite work is included only when stated in the SOW. Otherwise pre-approved travel is passed through at actual reasonable cost; onsite professional time is billed at the agreed rate/add-on. Receipts and allocation are retained.

## Tracking columns

Trip ID, cluster, dates, purpose, meetings, lead source, paid onsite revenue, airfare/rail, mileage, lodging, transit, meals, event, hours, opportunities, weighted pipeline, won revenue, gross profit, reimbursement status.
