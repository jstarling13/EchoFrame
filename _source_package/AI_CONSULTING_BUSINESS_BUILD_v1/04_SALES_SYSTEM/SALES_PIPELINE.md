# Sales Pipeline and Qualification

**Purpose:** Run a disciplined referral-to-close process

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Stages

Target → Introduced → Fit Call Scheduled → Qualified → Paid Diagnostic Proposed → Proposal Sent → Verbal Commit → Contract/Deposit → Won → Delivery → Expansion/Referral. Every record has owner, next action, next-action date, source, industry, need, budget range, decision process, risk flags, and lost reason.

## Qualification: CLEAR

**C**ost or consequence is material. **L**eader sponsors the work. **E**xisting workflow is observable and repeated. **A**ccess to people/data/systems is feasible. **R**isk and readiness fit a controlled engagement. Score each 0-2; 8+ qualified, 5-7 nurture/diagnostic only, below 5 disqualify.

## Exit criteria

A proposal is not sent until problem, baseline, sponsor, process owner, buying process, budget, data class, and next decision date are known.
