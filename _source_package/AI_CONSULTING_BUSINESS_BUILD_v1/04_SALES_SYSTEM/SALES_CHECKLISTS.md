# Sales Checklists

**Purpose:** Standardize opportunity handling

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Before discovery

Research company and role; confirm referral context; prepare hypothesis; avoid handling confidential data; send agenda.

## After discovery

Send recap in one business day; score CLEAR; record data/risk flags; select next step; schedule it.

## Before proposal

Baseline known; sponsor/process owner named; authority/budget/process known; security feasibility; scope and exclusions; success metrics; delivery capacity.

## Before close

Final SOW/MSA reviewed; legal entity and signer verified; payment method; deposit invoice/link; travel; kickoff; records saved.

## After close

Mark source; create project ID/folder; handoff sales notes; schedule intake; issue data handling instructions; do not begin before contract/deposit.
