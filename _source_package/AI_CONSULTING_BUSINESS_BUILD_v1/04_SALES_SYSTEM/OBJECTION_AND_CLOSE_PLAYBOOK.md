# Objection and Close Playbook

**Purpose:** Handle objections without pressure

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Price

Reconfirm measured consequence. Offer narrower scope, phased payment, or diagnostic. Do not reduce price without removing scope or documenting design-partner consideration.

## Security

Do not reassure vaguely. Offer data classification, approved-tool review, low-risk pilot, no-training/no-retention settings where available, access controls, test data, and written approval gates.

## Team resistance

Include employees in mapping, preserve judgment, show the before/after, pilot with champions, measure burden, and provide fallback.

## Close checklist

Sponsor and process owner named; SOW matches proposal; assumptions/exclusions clear; success criteria measurable; security gate complete; payment schedule consistent; travel approved; change control explained; signature and deposit received; kickoff scheduled.

## Lost deal process

Record primary/secondary reason, competitor/status quo, price/scope, timing, risk concern, and next revisit date. Send a courteous close note. Do not nurture indefinitely without a trigger.
