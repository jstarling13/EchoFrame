# Assumptions

**Purpose:** Make all material v1 assumptions visible

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Business

Founder-led United States consultancy; initial sales focus in New York; remote-first with batched onsite travel; 10-250 employee clients; vendor-neutral delivery; no claim of legal, medical, accounting, cybersecurity, or compliance certification.

## Commercial

USD pricing before sales tax, pass-through travel and third-party tools, signed SOW before work, deposits due before scheduling. O1 and O5 prepaid. O2 is 50/50. O3 is 40/30/30. O4 is 30/25/25/20.

## Technology

Vercel hosts the marketing/intake application; IONOS remains registrar/DNS operator unless the owner intentionally changes nameservers; Stripe handles payment collection; transactional email provider is selected during implementation.

## Risk

No sensitive data is submitted to AI systems by default. Client approval, appropriate vendor terms, access controls, and a documented data path are prerequisites. PHI use is prohibited until counsel/security confirm a compliant architecture and required agreements.

## Economics

Recovered time is capacity, not cash savings, unless payroll/overtime/contractor expenditure actually declines. Revenue opportunity is probability-weighted and reported separately.
