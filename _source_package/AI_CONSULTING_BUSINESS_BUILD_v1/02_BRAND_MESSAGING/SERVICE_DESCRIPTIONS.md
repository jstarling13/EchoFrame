# Service Descriptions

**Purpose:** Explain the five commercial offers in plain language

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Workflow Opportunity Diagnostic

**Best for:** Up to 3 departments, 8 interviews, 12 workflows inventoried. **Outcome:** Readiness score, ranked opportunity register, one current-state map, 90-day roadmap. **Timeline:** 10 business days. **Investment:** $2,500. **Next step:** Credit $1,000 toward O2 or O3 if signed within 30 days.

## Workflow Build Sprint

**Best for:** One department; up to 2 medium-complexity workflows and 10 users. **Outcome:** Workflow designs, configured solution, test evidence, SOPs, prompt library, admin handoff. **Timeline:** 4 weeks. **Investment:** $7,500. **Next step:** Apply reusable assets toward O3; transition to O5.

## AI Operations Transformation

**Best for:** Up to 3 departments, 5 workflows, 30 users. **Outcome:** Blueprint, implementation plan, implemented workflows, governance pack, training, ROI baseline, final handoff. **Timeline:** 8-10 weeks. **Investment:** $18,000. **Next step:** O5 support or O4 expansion.

## Enterprise AI Operations Program

**Best for:** Up to 6 departments, 12 workflows, 100 users; custom scope above this level. **Outcome:** Portfolio roadmap, governance, implementations, change management, train-the-trainer, ROI dashboard. **Timeline:** 16-20 weeks. **Investment:** $45,000. **Next step:** O5 Enterprise support; separately scoped expansion.

## Workflow Assurance & Enablement

**Best for:** Up to 8 support hours, 2 minor workflow changes, monitoring review. **Outcome:** Health report, issue log, improvements, office hours, quarterly value review. **Timeline:** Monthly; 3-month initial term. **Investment:** $2,500. **Next step:** Additional 10-hour block: $2,000; enterprise retainer from $5,000/month.
