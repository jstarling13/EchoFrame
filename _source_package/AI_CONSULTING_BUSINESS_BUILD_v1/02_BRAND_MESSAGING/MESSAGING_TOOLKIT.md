## 30-second pitch

Most companies do not need another AI demo. They need one real bottleneck mapped, improved, tested, and adopted. We do that work end to end and leave the owner with the workflow, controls, documentation, and trained people.

## 60-second pitch

We begin with how work moves today, not with a favorite tool. We identify where time, quality, or responsiveness is being lost, score the opportunities, and select the smallest useful workflow. Then we design the data path, choose the right model or automation, build and test it, train managers and employees, and measure what changed. Clients receive the SOPs, instruction library, admin access, and decision record. Ongoing support is available, but dependency is not the business model.
