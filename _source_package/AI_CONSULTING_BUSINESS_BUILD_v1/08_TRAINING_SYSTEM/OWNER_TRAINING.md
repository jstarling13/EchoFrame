# Owner Training Curriculum

**Purpose:** Build role-appropriate competency for owner participants

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Outcomes

Owner participants can explain approved uses, protect data, operate assigned workflows, verify outputs, handle exceptions, and locate support. Advanced participants can design and test changes within governance.

## Agenda

1. Business goal and workflow map (20 min)
2. Capabilities and limits (20 min)
3. Data and acceptable use (20 min)
4. Live workflow practice (35 min)
5. Verification, fallback, escalation (25 min)
6. Exercise and assessment (30 min)
7. Commitments and support (10 min)

## Exercise

Run one normal case, one incomplete case, one misleading-input case, and one sensitive-data case. Explain why the output is accepted, corrected, rejected, or escalated.

## Competency assessment

Pass requires 80% knowledge score plus successful observed operation: approved input, correct tool/account, verification, exception response, and no prohibited data handling. Remediate and reassess failed competencies.

## Worksheet

Workflow goal; trigger; approved inputs; prohibited inputs; steps; required review; source checks; failure signs; fallback; escalation contact; improvement idea; evidence needed before change.
