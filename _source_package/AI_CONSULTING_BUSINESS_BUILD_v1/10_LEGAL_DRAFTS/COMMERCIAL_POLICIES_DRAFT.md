# Commercial Policies — Draft

**Purpose:** DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW. This is a business-operating draft, not legal advice. Counsel must adapt it to the entity, jurisdiction, services, insurance, data practices, and client sector.

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Payment

Deposits and schedules follow the offer catalog/SOW. ACH may be preferred; card availability may vary. Client pays approved pass-through costs and applicable taxes.

## Cancellation

Rescheduling/cancellation affects reserved capacity. Draft: one reschedule with 5 business days’ notice; later changes may incur a fee or shift schedule. Project termination follows MSA/SOW.

## Refund

No blanket “no refunds.” Draft: fees for completed work, committed costs, and reserved capacity are earned as defined by counsel; unearned prepaid amounts are returned where contract/law requires. Stripe disputes do not replace contract notice.

## Travel

Remote-first. Onsite scope and professional-day fee in SOW. Pre-approved reasonable travel at actual cost, receipts retained, changes communicated.

## Change order

Written description, reason, scope effect, fee, schedule, risk/data effect, acceptance change, and signatures before changed work.

## Ownership transfer

Transfer client-owned accounts, configurations, bespoke paid deliverables, documentation, and exportable data subject to third-party terms and full payment. Retain no credentials; delete access on offboarding.
