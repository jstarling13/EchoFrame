# Mutual NDA — Draft

**Purpose:** DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW. This is a business-operating draft, not legal advice. Counsel must adapt it to the entity, jurisdiction, services, insurance, data practices, and client sector.

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Confidential information

Nonpublic business, technical, financial, client, security, personal, workflow, and product information disclosed in any form; oral disclosures should be identified and summarized when practical.

## Exclusions

Information independently developed, lawfully known, public without breach, or lawfully received without duty.

## Duties

Use only to evaluate/perform the relationship; restrict to need-to-know recipients under equivalent duty; use reasonable safeguards; no unauthorized AI/tool submission; notify of suspected unauthorized access.

## Compelled disclosure

Provide prompt notice where lawful and disclose only what is required.

## Term and return

Counsel selects confidentiality term and trade-secret treatment. On request, return/delete subject to backups/legal retention and confirm completion.

## No license

No IP license or obligation to transact. Remedies, governing law, residuals, reverse engineering, and publicity require counsel review.
