# Competitive Alternatives and Objection Framework

**Purpose:** Sell against the status quo and credible alternatives

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Alternatives

Do nothing; ask employees to experiment; buy a point solution; hire an automation freelancer; use incumbent IT/MSP; engage a large consultancy; hire an internal AI/ops lead.

## Response frame

Acknowledge → diagnose underlying risk → provide evidence/boundary → propose smallest safe next step.

## Common objections

**“We can do this ourselves.”** Agree; position O1 as acceleration and governance, with transfer as the goal. **“AI makes mistakes.”** Show test set, human approval, restricted actions, logging, and fallback. **“Our data is sensitive.”** Begin with classification and low-risk data; no tool use until controls are approved. **“No time.”** Start with one bottleneck and require a named owner; do not hide the client effort. **“Too expensive.”** Compare to verified cost, capacity, delay, and risk; reduce scope, not price integrity. **“Which model is best?”** Select per task using tests, not allegiance.
