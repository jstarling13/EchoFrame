# Positioning Architecture

**Purpose:** Keep messaging coherent by audience and funnel stage

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Category line

AI operations enablement for established businesses.

## Promise

Working workflows your team can operate, verify, and improve.

## Mechanism

Business-process mapping + vendor-neutral design + implementation + role-based training + ownership transfer.

## Evidence

Before/after maps, acceptance tests, adoption measures, ROI categories, SOPs, decision logs.

## Guardrails

No guaranteed savings, no autonomous consequential decisions by default, no silent sensitive-data processing, no false vendor neutrality.

## CTA ladder

Referral conversation → 20-minute fit call → paid diagnostic → build sprint/transformation → assurance retainer.
