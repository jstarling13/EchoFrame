# Industry Priorities

**Purpose:** Sequence initial New York vertical learning

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Wave 1

Accounting/bookkeeping operations (non-judgment workflows), commercial real estate/property management, construction and specialty contractors, recruiting/staffing, architecture/engineering administration, established B2B services. These combine document volume, repeated coordination, and reachable owner/operators.

## Wave 2

Insurance operations, hospitality groups, logistics/distribution, manufacturing administration, home services, dental/medical administration with non-PHI pilots.

## Delay or tightly gate

Legal advice generation, clinical decisions, patient PHI workflows, lending/underwriting, investment recommendations, employment screening/ranking, biometric surveillance, fully autonomous customer commitments, safety-critical operational control. Enter only with counsel, security design, human accountability, and sector-specific expertise.

## Expansion principle

Expand by workflow pattern and evidence, not by declaring permanent vertical specialization.
