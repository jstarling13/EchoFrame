# 30/60/90-Day Plan

**Purpose:** Launch a credible founder-led practice

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Days 1-30

Resolve name/entity/counsel; finalize five offers; implement website and CRM; configure Stripe test/live controls; create 50-account referral map; conduct 15 problem interviews; rehearse diagnostic; secure two paid design partners.

## Days 31-60

Deliver first diagnostic and build sprint; measure baseline and acceptance results; hold two small owner/operator roundtables; publish one anonymized workflow teardown; build five referral partnerships; tighten scope from delivery evidence.

## Days 61-90

Convert qualified clients to transformation/retainer; publish approved case study; target 10 qualified introductions/month; review win/loss data, utilization, margin, travel, and adoption; decide first contractor capability based on bottleneck.

## Stop rules

Do not add industries before one repeatable workflow pattern has evidence. Do not discount below a written design-partner exchange. Do not automate a risky process to win a deal.
