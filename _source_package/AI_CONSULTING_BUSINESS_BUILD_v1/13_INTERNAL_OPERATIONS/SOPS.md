# Internal SOP Collection

**Purpose:** Standardize onboarding through advocacy

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Onboarding

Verify contract/deposit; create records/folder; send secure data instructions; kickoff; confirm RACI, schedule, risks, approvals, baseline.

## Project

Weekly plan/status/risks/decisions; control scope; version artifacts; test before launch; invoice milestones; escalate blockers.

## Offboarding

Acceptance; ownership/access transfer; open items; support boundary; revoke access; deletion record; archive; financial reconciliation.

## Referral

Log source and permission; thank referrer without disclosing confidential outcome; no undisclosed compensation; request one specific introduction after value.

## Testimonial/case study

Wait for verified result; secure written approval of name, quote, metrics, screenshots, and channels; provide withdrawal/correction process as counsel directs.

## Travel/expense

Pre-approve; assign trip/project; retain receipts; separate reimbursable/nonreimbursable; reconcile monthly; do not mark personal costs as business.

## Bookkeeping recommendation

Separate business banking/card; sync Stripe payouts and fees; invoice IDs tied to project; categorize deposits/deferred revenue with CPA; monthly reconciliation; receipt capture; tax reserve; contractor W-9/1099 process; CPA review.
