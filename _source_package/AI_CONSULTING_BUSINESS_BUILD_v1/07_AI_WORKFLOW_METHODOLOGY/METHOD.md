# OWNED Workflow Method

**Purpose:** Define a vendor-neutral implementation method

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## O — Observe

Walk the real process; measure volume, time, waiting, rework, exceptions, and decision rights.

## W — Weigh

Score value, feasibility, data readiness, adoption, reuse, and risk. Classify benefits honestly.

## N — Navigate

Design the workflow and data path; compare manual, model-assisted, automated, API, knowledge-base, spreadsheet, CRM, and existing-software options.

## E — Engineer

Build the smallest controlled version. Specify instructions, tools, retrieval, integrations, human approval, validation, logging, fallback, and escalation.

## D — Demonstrate and transfer

Test against representative cases; train by role; launch with monitoring; document; transfer accounts and admin rights; measure 30/90-day impact.
