# Client Data Policy

**Purpose:** Establish a practical security baseline

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-08-28

## Policy

Collect minimum; approved purpose/location; client remains source owner; classify; access by need; no public-form sensitive data; no AI submission without authorization; retention/deletion schedule; incident escalation.

## Evidence

Record owner, approver, effective date, systems in scope, exceptions, control evidence, incident contact, and next review.

## Exception rule

Exceptions require written business reason, risk assessment, compensating controls, expiry date, and accountable approval. Convenience alone is not sufficient.
